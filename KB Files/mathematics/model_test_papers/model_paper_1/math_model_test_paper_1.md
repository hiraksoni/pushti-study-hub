### Achievers Section

#### Q46. [Algebraic Animal Legs] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Total legs $= 4(3c - 3) + 4(5p - 2) + 2(11d - 4) = 12c - 12 + 20p - 8 + 22d - 8 = 12c + 20p + 22d - 28$.

#### Q47. [Distributive Law Statements] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Statement-I is False ($43 \times (-100) = -4300 \neq 4300$). Statement-II is True ($p \times (q + r) = p \times q + p \times r$ is the distributive property of multiplication over addition).

#### Q48. [Vertically Opposite Angles] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $\Delta AOB \cong \Delta DOC \implies \angle AOB = \angle DOC = 76^\circ$. Angle sum in $\Delta AOB$ gives $\angle ODC = 80^\circ, \angle OCD = 24^\circ, \angle OCA = 66^\circ$.

#### Q49. [Virahanka (Fibonacci) Numbers] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: 6th Virahanka number $= 13$, 7th Virahanka number $= 21$. Required product $= 13 \times 21 = 273$.

#### Q50. [Central Tendency Audit] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Ordered data ($n=13$): 29, 29, 31, 32, 35, 35, 35, 36, 37, 38, 38, 41, 43. Median (7th term) $= 35$. Mean $= \frac{459}{13} \approx 35.30$. Range $= 43 - 29 = 14$.
