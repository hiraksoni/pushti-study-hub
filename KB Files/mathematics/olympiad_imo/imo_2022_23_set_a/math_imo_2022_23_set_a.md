#### Q47. [Linear Equations Statement Verification] (3 Marks)

Statement II: $m = 3n - 5$. If $m = 49 \implies 49 = 3n - 5 \implies 3n = 54 \implies n = 18$ (Statement says $n=49$, False). Statement I is true, II is false.

#### Q48. [Exponential Equations & Laws of Exponents] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**

(Q) $\frac{5^2 \times 35^2 \times 9^0}{49 \times 125 \times 45} = \frac{5^2 \times 5^2 \times 7^2 \times 1}{7^2 \times 5^3 \times 3^2 \times 5} = \frac{1}{3^2}$. (R) $(-1)^4 - (-1)^5 = 1 - (-1) = 2 = 2^0 \times 2^1$.

#### Q50. [Mensuration (Circle & Parallelogram)] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**

(b) Area of parallelogram $= \text{Base} \times \text{Height} \implies PQ \times SM = QR \times SN \implies 14 \times 4 = 8 \times SN \implies SN = \frac{56}{8} = 7\text{ cm}$.
