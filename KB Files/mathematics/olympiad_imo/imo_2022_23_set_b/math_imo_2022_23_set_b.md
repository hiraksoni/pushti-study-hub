### Logical Reasoning

#### Q1. [Figure Rotation Pattern] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: In each step, the centre element rotates $90^\circ$ clockwise; corner element shifts clockwise by one corner, increasing and decreasing by one alternately.

#### Q2. [Dictionary Word Ordering] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Dictionary order: Happy (2), Hazard (5), Health (1), Honour (3), Hungry (4) $\implies 2, 5, 1, 3, 4$.

#### Q3. [Mathematical Operations] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Evaluating substituted expression: $115 + 17 - 81 \div 3 \times 4 = 115 + 17 - 27 \times 4 = 115 + 17 - 108 = 24$.

#### Q4. [Dice Opposite Numbers] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Opposite face pairs on the folded dice net: $(1, 3), (2, 6), (4, 5)$.

#### Q5. [Geometric Pattern Transformation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: The second figure removes outermost element; third figure removes outermost element of the second figure.

#### Q6. [Mirror Reflection] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Lateral inversion of string `OLY#@M?PIC` across vertical mirror line.

#### Q7. [Triangle Counting] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Triangles formed: 10 individual unit triangles ($a$ through $j$), plus 3 compound 4-unit triangles ($abcd, defg, ghij$) $\implies$ total 13 triangles.
- **Diagram Reference**: *[Refer Textbook Page 150: Row of interlocking equilateral triangles]*

#### Q8. [Venn Diagrams (Astronomical Bodies)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Earth (planet), Jupiter (gas giant planet), and Sun (star) are completely distinct celestial bodies $\implies$ 3 separate non-overlapping circles.

#### Q9. [Coding-Decoding (-1 Shift)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Letters coded by diagonal cross $-1$ pattern: WONDROUS becomes N V C M N Q R T.

#### Q10. [Pattern Sequence Movement] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Elements rotate anti-clockwise to next corner and a new element is appended.
