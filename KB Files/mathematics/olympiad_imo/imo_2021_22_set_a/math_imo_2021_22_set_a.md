### Everyday Mathematics

#### Q36. [Area of Circular Path] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Inner radius $r = 3\text{ m}$, outer radius $R = 3 + 2.5 = 5.5\text{ m}$. Area of circular path $= \pi(R^2 - r^2) = 3.14(5.5^2 - 3^2) = 3.14 \times 21.25 = 66.725\text{ m}^2$.

#### Q37. [Linear Equations (Ages)] (1 Mark)
