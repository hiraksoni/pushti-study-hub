## 1. Chapter Overview & Core Theoretical Principles

> **Syllabus Scope**: Introduction to letters as generalized numbers (variables), forming expressions, constants, terms, coefficients, like and unlike terms, matchstick patterns, and evaluating expressions by substitution.

### Variables and Constants
- **Variable**: A letter or symbol ($x, y, a, b$) that can take different numerical values.
- **Constant**: A fixed numerical quantity ($7, -3, \frac{1}{2}$).
- **Algebraic Expression**: Combination of variables, constants, and fundamental operations ($+, -, \times, \div$).

### Terms and Coefficients
In $4x^2 - 7xy + 9$: Terms are $4x^2$, $-7xy$, $9$. Coefficient of $4x^2$ is $4$; of $-7xy$ is $-7$.

### Like vs Unlike Terms
- **Like Terms**: Same algebraic factors: e.g. $3xy$ and $-5xy$.
- **Unlike Terms**: Different algebraic factors: e.g. $3x$ and $3y$.

#### [MTG_MATH4_MCQ_011] If each 'T' and each 'L' requires 2 matchsticks, and a student makes $t$ T's and $l$ L's, then the expression for total matchsticks used is:
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Algebraic Expressions` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
  - (a) $t + l$
  - (b) $2t + 2l$
  - (c) $t + 2l$
  - (d) $2
- **Answer**: **$t + l$**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: Each 'T' needs 2 matchsticks ($2t$) and each 'L' needs 2 matchsticks ($2l$). Total matchsticks = $2t + 2l$ or $2(t + l)$.

#### [MTG_MATH4_MCQ_018] Which of the following expressions is equivalent to $6(2a - 3b)$?

#### [MTG_MATH4_MCQ_019] Which of the following pairs contains UNLIKE terms?
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Algebraic Expressions` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
  - (a) $7x$ and $-2x$
  - (b) $4ab$ and $-3ba$
  - (c) $5x^2y$ and $5xy^2$
  - (d) $12$ and $-8$
- **Answer**: **$7x$ and $-2x$**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: In $5x^2y$, the variable factors are $x \times x \times y$. In $5xy^2$, the variable factors are $x \times y \times y$. Because the powers do not match, they are unlike terms.

#### [MTG_MATH4_MCQ_020] The perimeter of a rectangle of length $(7x - 3y)$ and breadth $(2x + y)$ is:
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Algebraic Expressions` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
  - (a) $9x - 2y$
  - (b) $18x - 4y$
  - (c) $14x - 6y$
  - (d) $18x - 2y$
- **Answer**: **$9x - 2y$**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: $\text{Perimeter} = 2(\text{length} + \text{breadth}) = 2[(7x - 3y) + (2x + y)] = 2[9x - 2y] = 18x - 4y$.

#### [MTG_MATH4_MCQ_021] Subtract $(3xy + 5yz - 7zx)$ from $(5xy - 2yz - 2zx)$:
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Algebraic Expressions` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
  - (a) $2xy - 7yz + 5zx$
  - (b) $-2xy + 7yz - 5zx$
  - (c) $2xy + 3yz - 9zx$
  - (d) $8xy + 3yz - 9zx$
- **Answer**: **$2xy - 7yz + 5zx$**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: $(5xy - 2yz - 2zx) - (3xy + 5yz - 7zx) = (5 - 3)xy + (-2 - 5)yz + (-2 + 7)zx = 2xy - 7yz + 5zx$.

#### [MTG_MATH4_MCQ_026] In a $2 \times 2$ calendar grid of dates $\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$, if $a$ is the top-left date, what is the sum of the two diagonal pairs $(a + d)$ and $(b + c)$?
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Algebraic Expressions` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
  - (a) $a + d$ is always 7 more than $b + c$
  - (b) $a + d$ is equal to $b + c$
  - (c) $a + d$ is 14 more than $b + c$
  - (d) It depends on the month
- **Answer**: **$a + d$ is always 7 more than $b + c$**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: In a $2 \times 2$ calendar square: $b = a + 1$, $c = a + 7$, $d = a + 8$. Then $a + d = a + (a + 8) = 2a + 8$, and $b + c = (a + 1) + (a + 7) = 2a + 8$. The two diagonal sums are always exactly equal!

#### [MTG_MATH4_MCQ_027] Simplify: $-3(x - 2y) + 2(2x - y) - 5(x - y)$:
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Algebraic Expressions` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`

  - (b) $-4x + 7y$
  - (c) $-4x - 9y$
  - (d) $4x + 9y$
- **Answer**: **$-4x + 9y$**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: Expand each term: $-3x + 6y + 4x - 2y - 5x + 5y = (-3 + 4 - 5)x + (6 - 2 + 5)y = -4x + 9y$.

#### [MTG_MATH4_MCQ_028] Subtract $(2a - 3b + 4c)$ from the sum of $(a + b + c)$ and $(a - 2b + 3c)$:
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Algebraic Expressions` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
  - (a) $4c$
  - (b) $2b$
  - (c) $0$
  - (d) $-2b$
- **Answer**: **$4c$**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: Sum of the two: $(a + a) + (b - 2b) + (c + 3c) = 2a - b + 4c$. Now subtract: $(2a - b + 4c) - (2a - 3b + 4c) = (2a - 2a) + (-b + 3b) + (4c - 4c) = 2b$.
