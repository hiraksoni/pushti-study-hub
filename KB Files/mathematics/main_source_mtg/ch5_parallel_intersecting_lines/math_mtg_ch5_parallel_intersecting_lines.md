## 1. Chapter Overview & Core Theoretical Principles

> **Syllabus Scope**: Geometric properties of lines, rays, line segments, intersecting lines, vertically opposite angles, transversals intersecting parallel lines, and corresponding/alternate angle theorems.

### Intersecting Lines & Vertically Opposite Angles
When two lines intersect, vertically opposite angles are equal: $\angle 1 = \angle 3$ and $\angle 2 = \angle 4$.

### Transversal Intersecting Parallel Lines
When a transversal intersects parallel lines:
1. Corresponding Angles are equal.
2. Alternate Interior Angles are equal.
3. Co-Interior Angles are supplementary ($180^\circ$).
