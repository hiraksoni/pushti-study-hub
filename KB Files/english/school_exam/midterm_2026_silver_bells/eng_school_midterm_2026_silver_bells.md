## Section A: Reading (20 Marks)

### Q1. Unseen Passage 1: The Elderly Carpenter and the House (10 Marks)
- **A. Why did the elderly carpenter build the final house poorly?**
  - **Answer:** (ii) He was eager to retire and did not put his full effort and care into the project.
  - **Explanation:** Text explicitly mentions his heart was not in his work and he resorted to shoddy workmanship out of impatience to retire.
- **B. What surprise does the employer reveal to the carpenter?**
  - **Answer:** (iv) He gives the keys of the house to the carpenter as a retirement gift.
- **C. Antonym of "shoddy":**
  - **Answer:** (iii) Superior. (Shoddy = poor quality; Superior = high quality).
- **D. Symbolism of the carpenter's embarrassment:**
  - **Answer:** (iii) The realization that we must live with the consequences of the choices we make today.
- **E. Primary moral lesson:**
  - **Answer:** (ii) Always do your best and act with integrity, because you are ultimately building your own future.
- **F. What did the carpenter tell his employer?**
  - **Answer:** He told his employer of his plans to retire from house building and live a leisurely life with his family.
- **G. What favour did his employer ask?**
  - **Answer:** The employer asked him to build just one more house as a personal favour.
- **H. Wood chosen for rebuilding in retirement:**
  - **Answer:** Strong oak.
- **I. Two things that build our future "house":**
  - **Answer:** Our attitude and the choices we make today.
- **J. Word in paragraph 1 meaning 'art of skill of a workman':**
  - **Answer:** Workmanship.

### Q2. Unseen Passage 2: Australia's 2019–20 Bushfires (10 Marks)
- **a. Confirmation about the 2019–20 fires:** (i) the fires were not normal.
- **b. Difference in recorded temperatures:** (iv) 2019 recorded the warmest temperatures since 1880s.
- **c. Hiroshima comparison with rising sea temperatures:** (ii) rising at the equivalent of five Hiroshima bombs a second.
- **d. Antonym of 'fragile':** (ii) Robust.
- **e. Correct statement:** (iv) Pascal Peduzzi is the Director of UNEP’s Global Resource Information Database.
- **f. Steps recommended by UN experts:** Immediate coordinated global action, transitioning away from fossil fuels, and investing in tech-driven early warning systems.
- **g. Who said "our planet is burning":** UN Secretary-General António Guterres.
- **h. Word in paragraph 6 meaning "original":** Indigenous (or Traditional).
- **i. What forced conservationists to rethink strategies:** The destruction of critical habitats pushing fragile ecosystems to the brink.
- **j. How wildlife/wildfire data is analysed:** Sliced and diced by month, type of land cover, protected area, province, and nation.
