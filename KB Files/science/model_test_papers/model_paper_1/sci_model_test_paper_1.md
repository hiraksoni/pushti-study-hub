### Science

#### Q11. [Uniform Speed from Graph] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Straight linear slope on position-time graph between $t_1$ and $t_2$ indicates uniform constant speed.

#### Q12. [Closed Electric Circuits] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Only circuit arrangement D provides an unbroken conducting loop from positive to negative battery terminal.

#### Q13. [Clinical Thermometer Range] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: A human clinical thermometer is calibrated from $35^\circ\text{C}$ to $42^\circ\text{C}$ (covering normal physiological range $37^\circ\text{C}$).

#### Q14. [Units of Length Measurement] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $1.5\text{ m} = 150\text{ cm}$ is the shortest, while $179\text{ cm}$ is the longest.

#### Q15. [Multiple Reflections ($60^\circ$)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Two mirrors inclined at $60^\circ$ form $\frac{360^\circ}{60^\circ} - 1 = 5$ symmetrical images.

#### Q16. [Astronomical Leap Year] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Earth revolves in $365.25\text{ days}$. The extra $0.25\text{ day}$ accumulates every 4 years to add 1 full day (Feb 29).

#### Q17. [Total Solar Eclipse] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: The Moon's umbra shadow covers only a narrow strip on Earth where total solar eclipse occurs.

#### Q18. [Magnetic Separation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Magnets separate ferromagnetic metals (iron, nickel) from non-magnetic materials (copper, aluminum, plastics).

#### Q19. [Optics (Shadow Formation)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Shadow shape depends on angle of illumination and object orientation; it may not resemble the exact 3D outline.

#### Q20. [Clinical Thermometer Kink] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: A constriction/kink prevents mercury from falling back before reading; laboratory thermometers have no kink.

#### Q21. [Indicator Battery] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: China rose turns dark pink in acid; turmeric turns red in base; phenolphthalein turns pink in base; methyl orange turns red in acid.

#### Q22. [Optical Transmission Properties] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Air is transparent, chiffon cloth is translucent, wood is opaque, and an active bulb is luminous.

#### Q23. [Charring of Sugar] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Heating sugar decomposes sucrose into black carbon and water vapour (chemical change). Heating copper sulphate is physical/reversible.

#### Q24. [Soil pH Amendment] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Acidic soils are treated with quicklime (CaO) or slaked lime $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$; basic soils are treated with decaying organic manure.

#### Q25. [Antacid Neutralisation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Weak bases like sodium bicarbonate $\text{NaHCO}_3$ and milk of magnesia $\text{Mg(OH)}_2$ neutralize excess hydrochloric acid in gastric juice.

#### Q26. [Separation of Sand, Water, Salt] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Filtration traps insoluble sand Z on filter paper; evaporation of filtrate yields common salt X; condensed liquid is water Y.

#### Q27. [Phase Transitions of Water] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Boiling into steam (vaporisation) and condensing on a cold metal plate are physical, reversible changes.

#### Q28. [Enzymatic Browning of Apple] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Polyphenol oxidase in exposed apple flesh reacts with atmospheric oxygen, forming brown melanin pigments (oxidation).

#### Q29. [Insectivorous Nutrition] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: *Nepenthes* (pitcher plant) carries out autotrophic photosynthesis and heterotrophic insect digestion for nitrogen.

#### Q30. [Iodine Starch Test] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Decolourised leaf boiled in alcohol turns blue-black upon adding iodine solution, confirming the presence of starch produced by photosynthesis.

#### Q31. [Scientific Methodology] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Scientific theories evolve, refine, and expand continuously as new experimental evidence and technologies emerge.

#### Q32. [Polar Bear Thermal Adaptations] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Streamlined body aids swimming; 2 dense fur coats and a thick layer of subcutaneous fat (blubber) trap body warmth.

#### Q33. [Respiratory Gas Composition] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Inhaled air: $21\% \text{ O}_2, 0.04\% \text{ CO}_2$. Exhaled air: $16.4\% \text{ O}_2, 4.4\% \text{ CO}_2$.

#### Q34. [Vascular Transport] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Xylem translocates water/minerals from roots; phloem translocates sucrose; pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood to lungs.

#### Q35. [Adolescent Laryngeal Growth] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Rapid enlargement of thyroid cartilage of larynx (Adam's apple) during male puberty causes voice cracks and deepening.

#### Q36. [Secondary Sexual Changes] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Deepening of the voice occurs prominently in boys during puberty due to vocal cord thickening.

#### Q37. [Potometer Function] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: A Ganong's potometer measures the rate of water absorption which is nearly equal to the rate of transpiration in a leafy shoot.

#### Q38. [Stomatal Anatomy] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: A stoma is bounded by two kidney-shaped guard cells that swell with turgor to open the pore for gaseous exchange and transpiration.

#### Q39. [Nutritional Pathology] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Anaemia (iron deficiency/weakness), Kwashiorkor (protein deficiency/swollen abdomen), Rickets (Vit D deficiency/bent bones), Goitre (iodine deficiency).

#### Q40. [Human Basal Breathing Rate] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Normal resting respiratory rate of a healthy adult human is $15-20\text{ breaths per minute}$.

#### Q41. [Seed Germination Prerequisites] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Germination strictly requires water (hydrates enzymes), oxygen (cellular respiration), and suitable temperature (warmth).

#### Q42. [Ascorbic Acid (Vitamin C)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Deficiency of Vitamin C causes scurvy (bleeding gums, petechiae, slow wound healing). Rich sources: amla, lemons, oranges.

#### Q43. [Xerophytic Adaptations] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Leaves reduced to spines reduce transpiration; stems are fleshy green and covered with thick waxy cuticles to store water.

#### Q44. [Edible Plant Morphology] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Radish/carrot are tap roots; wheat/peas are seeds; tomato/mango are fruits; cauliflower/broccoli are immature inflorescences; cabbage is a leaf.

#### Q45. [Acid Precipitation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Atmospheric $\text{SO}_2$ and $\text{NO}_2$ dissolve in rain forming $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ and $\text{HNO}_3$, lowering environmental water pH.


### Achievers Section

#### Q46. [Thermal Conduction in Paper Wrap] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Paper wrapped tightly on a copper rod does not burn because copper rapidly conducts heat away; paper wrapped on plastic insulator burns.

#### Q47. [Chemical Reaction Suite] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Zinc displaces copper: $\text{CuSO}_4 + \text{Zn} \to \text{ZnSO}_4 + \text{Cu}$. Rusting: $\text{Fe} + \text{O}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 \cdot x\text{H}_2\text{O}$. Endothermic $\text{C} + \text{S} \to \text{CS}_2$.

#### Q48. [Photosynthesis vs Respiration] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: In dark, plants do not carry out photosynthesis but respire continuously, consuming $\text{O}_2$ and releasing $\text{CO}_2$ which turns limewater milky.

#### Q49. [Digestive Enzymes Optimum pH] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Pepsin operates in acidic stomach ($	ext{pH} \approx 1.5-2$); intestinal trypsin operates in alkaline medium ($	ext{pH} \approx 7.8-8.5$). Bile contains no digestive enzymes.

#### Q50. [Pubertal Sexual Dimorphism] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Observed only in boys: Adam's apple, facial hair, voice deepening. Observed only in girls: breast development, pelvic widening, menstruation. Common: height increase, acne, pubic hair.
