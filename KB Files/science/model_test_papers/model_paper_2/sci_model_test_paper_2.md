### Science

#### Q11. [Thermal Radiation (Vacuum)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Thermal radiation transfers energy via electromagnetic infrared waves requiring zero material medium.

#### Q12. [Thermal Conductivity of Vessels] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Container P loses heat faster than Q, showing P is a superior thermal conductor and radiator.

#### Q13. [Boiling Point Thermodynamics] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Water boils at constant temperature $100^\circ\text{C}$ at $1\text{ atm}$. Different volumes absorb different quantities of latent heat.

#### Q14. [Systematic Error in Temperature] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: If both initial and final temperatures are measured with a $+1^\circ\text{C}$ systematic calibration offset, the temperature difference $\Delta T$ is exact (zero error).

#### Q15. [Plane Mirror Image Properties] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Images in a plane mirror are virtual, erect, laterally inverted, same size as object, and formed at equal distance behind mirror.

#### Q16. [Laws of Reflection] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: By Fermat's principle and laws of reflection, a single incident ray reflects as exactly one reflected ray.

#### Q17. [Electrical Insulators] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Dry wood (matchstick) has very high electrical resistance and acts as an insulator, breaking circuit current flow.

#### Q18. [Time Units Conversion] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $1\text{ hour} = 60\text{ minutes} = 60 \times 60 = 3600\text{ seconds}$.

#### Q19. [Measurement Comparison] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Direct linear scale comparison shows purple pencil is longest and green is shortest.

#### Q20. [Applications of Permanent Magnets] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Magnetic stripes on ATM debit cards, magnetic recording tapes in video cassettes, and compass needles use ferromagnetism.

#### Q21. [Corrosion Kinetics] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Rusting requires both water and dissolved oxygen. In dry air (tube X) or boiled deoxygenated water with oil seal (tube Z), iron does not rust.

#### Q22. [Material Sources] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Crockery (glass/ceramic), CD (polycarbonate/aluminum), cart (wood), pitcher (clay), book (cellulose paper).

#### Q23. [Limewater Basicity] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Limewater $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ is an aqueous alkaline base that dissociates hydroxide ions, turning red litmus blue.

#### Q24. [Evaporation of Seawater] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Common salt is harvested from seawater in shallow pans by solar evaporation of water solvent.

#### Q25. [Reactivity Series of Metals] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Reactivity: $\text{Zn} > \text{Fe} > \text{Cu} > \text{Au}$. Zinc displaces iron from green $\text{FeSO}_4$ to colourless $\text{ZnSO}_4$. Copper and gold cannot displace iron.

#### Q26. [Magnesium Oxide Chemistry] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $2\text{Mg} + \text{O}_2 \to 2\text{MgO}$; $\text{MgO} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{Mg(OH)}_2$ (basic, turns red litmus blue).

#### Q27. [Physical vs Chemical in Cooking] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Cracking an egg shell is physical. Cooking an omelette denatures egg albumin proteins irreversibly (chemical change).

#### Q28. [Household Acids and Bases] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Soft drinks contain carbonic acid; soap contains $\text{NaOH}$; curd contains lactic acid; toothpaste contains basic calcium carbonate.

#### Q29. [Biochemical Fruit Ripening] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Fruit ripening driven by ethylene gas alters sugars, pectins, and acids, constituting an irreversible chemical process.

#### Q30. [Electrical Conductors and Insulators] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Silver has the highest electrical conductivity of any metal; dry wood and plastic polymers are insulators.

#### Q31. [Bread Mould (*Rhizopus*)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: *Rhizopus* is a multicellular filamentous zygomycete fungus undergoing aerobic cellular respiration to break down glucose into $\text{CO}_2$ and energy.

#### Q32. [Female Reproductive Cycle] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Menarche marks the first menstrual cycle during female puberty; menopause marks the permanent cessation of ovarian follicular activity.

#### Q33. [Comparative Invertebrate Respiration] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Insects breathe via spiracles and tracheal branching; earthworms breathe cutaneously across moist cuticle; fish breathe via gills.

#### Q34. [Xerophytic Spines] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Cacti modify leaves into sharp spines to minimize surface area for transpiration and defend against herbivores.

#### Q35. [Carbon Dioxide Fire Extinguishers] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $\text{CO}_2$ is denser than air, does not support combustion, and blankets flames to displace atmospheric oxygen.

#### Q36. [Nutritional Pairings] (1 Mark)

#### Q37. [Dairy and Plant Foods] (1 Mark)

#### Q38. [Xylem and Phloem Functions] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Xylem transports water and dissolved inorganic ions unidirectionally upwards; phloem translocates organic nutrients bidirectionally.

#### Q39. [Nutrition Taxonomy] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Venus flytrap (insectivorous), mushroom (saprotrophic), *Cuscuta* (parasitic), lichen (symbiotic mutualism), green algae (autotrophic).

#### Q40. [Amphibian Respiration] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Adult frogs respire cutaneously through moist vascular skin in water, and via buccopharyngeal cavity and lungs on land.

#### Q41. [Adolescent Nutrition] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Fast foods are rich in refined fats and sodium but lack essential micronutrients, vitamins, and dietary fibre needed for growth.

#### Q42. [Biochemical Food Reagents] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Starch tested with iodine (blue-black); reducing sugars with Benedict's (brick-red); lipids with ethanol emulsion; proteins with Biuret (violet/purple).

#### Q43. [Plant Tropisms (Phototropism)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Plant shoots show positive phototropism (bend toward light via auxin redistribution); roots show negative phototropism.

#### Q44. [Five Stages of Holozoic Nutrition] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Ingestion (intake) $\to$ Digestion (enzymatic breakdown) $\to$ Absorption (mucosal uptake) $\to$ Assimilation (cellular incorporation) $\to$ Egestion (defecation).

#### Q45. [Vascular Transport Directionality] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Xylem transport is strictly unidirectional (upward via transpiration pull); phloem transport is bidirectional (source to sink).
