### Science

#### Q11. [Reflection of Light & Geometry] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Given angle $x = 20^\circ$. $y = 180^\circ - 120^\circ - 20^\circ = 40^\circ$. Angle of incidence $i = 90^\circ - y = 90^\circ - 40^\circ = 50^\circ$.

#### Q12. [Magnetic Induction in Iron Pins] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Opposite magnetic poles are induced at pointed tips of suspended iron pins, causing them to attract each other.

#### Q13. [Plane Mirror Image Distance] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Object distance equals image distance. Distance between Man A' and Man B' $= 5 + 2 + 2 = 9\text{ m}$.

#### Q14. [Thermal Convection Currents] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Heated fluid expands, becomes less dense, and rises; cooler, denser fluid sinks to replace it, establishing convection loop C.

#### Q15. [Electric Switch Routing] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Closing switches P, R, and T establishes a closed circuit exclusively for bulb Y, so only bulb Y glows.

#### Q16. [Mirror Rotation Theorem] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: When a plane mirror is rotated by an angle $\theta$, the reflected ray rotates through $2\theta$ in the same direction ($2 \times 10^\circ = 20^\circ$ anticlockwise).

#### Q17. [Thermometer Linear Scale Calibration] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $\frac{L_T - L_0}{L_{100} - L_0} = \frac{T - 0}{100 - 0} \implies \frac{23 - 9}{44 - 9} = \frac{\theta}{100} \implies \frac{14}{35} = \frac{\theta}{100} \implies \theta = 40^\circ\text{C}$.

#### Q18. [Cyclones & Atmospheric Storms] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Tornado speed $\approx 300\text{ km/h}$; Cyclone calm eye diameter $\approx 10-30\text{ km}$; Cloud wall region $\approx 150\text{ km}$; Cloud region wind speed $\approx 150-200\text{ km/h}$.

#### Q19. [Domestic Electric Wiring Safety] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Safety switches and fuses must always be connected in series with the live wire (not neutral wire) to isolate appliances from high voltage when turned off.

#### Q20. [Magnetic Polarity of Circular Loop] (1 Mark)

#### Q21. [Acids vs Bases in Daily Life] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Spinach juice (oxalic acid) and apple juice (malic acid) are acidic. Baking soda, bleaching powder, and detergents are basic.

#### Q22. [Classification of Changes] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Drying wet clothes is physical, desirable, and slow. Fruit ripening, plant growth, and burning candles involve irreversible chemical changes.

#### Q23. [Material Properties] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**

#### Q24. [Dilution of Concentrated Acids] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Diluting concentrated acid is violently exothermic. Always add acid slowly down the sides of the beaker into water with constant stirring; never add water into acid.

#### Q25. [Physical Irreversible Changes] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Breaking a glass tumbler alters physical shape without creating new chemical bonds; however, shattered fragments cannot be reassembled spontaneously (physical, irreversible).

#### Q26. [Magnesium Oxide & Indicators] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $2\text{Mg} + \text{O}_2 \to 2\text{MgO}$; $\text{MgO} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{Mg(OH)}_2$. Magnesium hydroxide is basic: turns red litmus blue, China rose green, methyl orange yellow, and leaves blue litmus unchanged.

#### Q27. [Chemical Formulas] (1 Mark)

### Achievers Section

#### Q46. [Displacement-Time Graph Velocity] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Slope of displacement-time graph gives instantaneous velocity. Slope decreases from O to P, becomes zero, then increases from P to Q. If initial and final positions coincide, net displacement is zero, yielding average velocity $= 0$.

#### Q47. [Chemical Reaction Triple Scheme] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Test tube I: Single displacement $\text{Fe} + \text{CuSO}_4 \to \text{FeSO}_4 + \text{Cu}$. Test tube II: Neutralisation $\text{NaHCO}_3 + \text{CH}_3\text{COOH} \to \text{CH}_3\text{COONa} + \text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$. Test tube III: Iron oxidation forming hydrated iron oxide rust $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 \cdot x\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (basic metal oxide).

#### Q48. [Leukocyte Morphology & Function] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Eosinophils and basophils are polymorphonuclear granulocytes defending against parasitic infections and mediating allergic inflammatory responses. Erythrocytes (RBCs) lack nuclei and transport respiratory gases.

#### Q49. [Floral Biology & Cross-Pollination] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Mustard has complete bisexual flowers undergoing cross-pollination. Cucumber has unisexual male and female flowers. Yeast is a budding unicellular fungus. *Oxalis* produces cleistogamous complete flowers that self-pollinate without opening.

#### Q50. [Penguin Physiology & Osmoregulation] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Penguins display countershading camouflage (white belly hides against sky from below, dark back hides against ocean depths from above). Supraorbital salt glands located above the orbits excrete excess sodium chloride from ingested seawater.
