### Science

#### Q11. [Heat Transfer & Ignition Temperature] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Heat supplied to the paper pot is conducted rapidly through the thin paper to water. The boiling point of water ($100^\circ\text{C}$) is far lower than the ignition temperature of paper, so paper does not burn.

#### Q12. [Friction & Kinematics] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Between points Y and Z the surface is completely smooth, meaning zero friction acts on the car. In the absence of an opposing force, velocity remains constant (Newton's First Law).

#### Q13. [Electric Circuits & Polarity] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Statement D is incorrect because reversing batteries in the manner shown disrupts net EMF and circuit current flow.

#### Q14. [Electrical Safety (Earthing)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Earthing provides a low-resistance path to discharge leakage current safely into the ground, protecting users from lethal electric shocks.

#### Q15. [Plane Mirrors & Field of View] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Light rays from object P reflect off the mirror into the observer's eye, making object P visible.

#### Q16. [Laws of Reflection] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Angle with surface is $30^\circ \implies$ Angle of incidence $\angle i = 90^\circ - 30^\circ = 60^\circ$. By law of reflection, $\angle r = \angle i = 60^\circ$.

#### Q17. [Displacement-Time Graphs] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Parallel lines on a displacement-time graph indicate identical slopes, meaning equal velocities. Since P was initially $20\text{ m}$ behind Q, P remains $20\text{ m}$ behind Q at all times.

#### Q18. [Optics (Lenses)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: A light ray travelling parallel to the principal axis of a convex lens refracts through the principal focus on the opposite side.

#### Q19. [Magnetic Fields & Neutral Points] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Between two opposing magnet poles oriented symmetrically, magnetic field lines cancel out at the midpoint Q, resulting in zero net magnetic field.

#### Q20. [Winds, Storms & Cyclones (Bernoulli Effect)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: High-speed winds are accompanied by reduced air pressure (Bernoulli's principle).

#### Q21. [Natural Indicators (China Rose)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: China rose indicator turns dark pink/magenta in acidic solutions and green in basic solutions. Calamine lotion is basic (contains zinc carbonate) so it turns green; lemon juice is acidic (citric acid) so it turns magenta.

#### Q22. [Physical and Chemical Changes] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Boiling an egg denatures and coagulates proteins irreversibly; raw egg cannot be recovered.

#### Q23. [Acids and Bases Chemistry] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Acids release hydrogen ions ($H^+$) in aqueous solution and have a characteristic sour taste.

#### Q24. [Chemical Reactions in Daily Life] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Enzymatic browning of cut brinjal, baking a cake, and cellular respiration are chemical and irreversible. Crystallisation and dissolution of sugar are physical and reversible.

#### Q25. [Allotropes of Carbon (Diamond)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Diamond has a rigid, densely linked 3D tetrahedral giant covalent lattice, making it one of the hardest natural substances.

#### Q26. [Characteristics of Chemical Changes] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Chemical changes produce entirely new substances with altered chemical properties and are generally irreversible.

#### Q27. [Separation of Substances] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Sedimentation allows heavier insoluble particles (chalk powder) to settle, followed by decantation pouring off clear water.

#### Q28. [Chemical Indicators Master Suite] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Methyl orange: red in acid, yellow in base. China rose: magenta in acid, green in base. Turmeric: stays yellow in acid/neutral, turns red in base.

#### Q29. [Connective Tissues (Ligaments)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Ligaments are tough, elastic connective tissues that connect bone to bone at joints.

#### Q30. [Waste Management & Sanitation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Incineration at high temperatures is the standard medical disposal method to destroy pathogens and sterilize biohazardous hospital waste.

#### Q31. [Fibre to Fabric (Ginning & Retting)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Ginning separates cotton fibres from seed pods; retting submerges jute stalks in water to decompose pectin and loosen fibres.

#### Q32. [Animal Adaptations & Habitats] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Reindeer are adapted to Arctic tundra/polar climates with dense fur and hooves; zebras are adapted to open African grasslands/savannas.

#### Q33. [Human Nutrition (Vitamins & Minerals)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Fresh fruits provide essential dietary vitamins (Vitamin C, A) and minerals that protect eyes, gums, skin, and strengthen immunity.

#### Q34. [Natural vs Synthetic Fibres] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Silk is an animal protein fibre with triangular prism-like fibroin structure giving smooth sheen; it has tensile strength comparable to steel wire.

#### Q35. [Human Respiratory System Pathway] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Anatomical order: Nostrils $\to$ Nasal cavity $\to$ Pharynx $\to$ Larynx/Trachea $\to$ Bronchi $\to$ Bronchioles $\to$ Alveoli.

#### Q36. [Properties of Air & Atmosphere] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Playing a flute (vibrating air column), blowing soap bubbles (surface tension & enclosed air), and flying kites (aerodynamic lift) all require air.

#### Q37. [Seed Dispersal by Wind] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Dandelion seeds possess feathery parachute tufts of hair (pappus) that catch gentle breezes for long-distance wind dispersal.

#### Q38. [Excretory Systems & Waste Products] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Ammonotelic organisms (aquatic animals, leeches, sponges) excrete toxic ammonia; ureotelic (sharks, frogs, mammals) excrete urea; uricotelic (lizards, birds) excrete uric acid paste.

#### Q39. [Insectivorous Plants (Nepenthes)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Pitcher plants contain chlorophyll and carry out photosynthesis (autotrophic), but trap and digest insects in modified leaf pitchers to obtain nitrogen in nutrient-poor soils (heterotrophic).

#### Q40. [Legumes & Nitrogen Assimilation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Beans, grams, and lentils are leguminous crops hosting symbiotic *Rhizobium* in root nodules to fix atmospheric nitrogen.

#### Q41. [Wool Processing (Shearing)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Shearing is the process of removing woolly fleece along with a thin outermost layer of dead skin cells from sheep without harming them.

#### Q42. [Biomes & Global Biodiversity] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Tropical rainforests support elephants, toucans, and red-eyed tree frogs. Seals and penguins inhabit polar waters; camels and fennec foxes inhabit deserts.

#### Q43. [Forest Ecosystems & Carbon Cycle] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Deforestation eliminates primary carbon sinks, leading to accumulating atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$, greenhouse effect, global warming, glacier melting, and loss of biodiversity.

#### Q44. [Circulatory System (Pulmonary Circuit)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Pulmonary circulation: Deoxygenated blood is pumped from the right ventricle through pulmonary arteries to the lungs, and oxygenated blood returns via pulmonary veins to the left atrium.

#### Q45. [Comparative Animal Respiration] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Leech: cutaneous respiration through moist skin. Grasshopper: tracheal tubes with spiracles. Fish: branchial respiration using gills.


### Achievers Section

#### Q46. [Motion Graph Analysis (Average Speed)] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Total time $t = 4\text{ min} = 4 \times 60 = 240\text{ s}$. Total distance $d = 20\text{ m}$. Average Speed $= \frac{20\text{ m}}{240\text{ s}} = \frac{1}{12}\text{ m/s} \approx 0.083\text{ m/s}$.

#### Q47. [Multi-Change Classification Matrix] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Batter to idli: chemical, irreversible. Melting of wax: physical, reversible. Rusting of iron: chemical, irreversible. Glowing of bulb: physical, reversible. Tearing of paper: physical, irreversible.

#### Q48. [Endocrine vs Exocrine Glands Venn Model] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Salivary glands secrete saliva via ducts into the mouth (exocrine only). Adrenal glands are purely ductless endocrine glands. Pancreas is a heterocrine (mixed) gland.

#### Q49. [Forest Canopy Stratification] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Stratification layers: Emergent layer $\to$ Canopy $\to$ Understory $\to$ Forest floor. The dense overlapping tree crowns of the canopy filter out 98-99% of sunlight, leaving the forest floor in deep shade.

#### Q50. [Asexual Modes of Reproduction] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: *Spirogyra* reproduces vegetatively by fragmentation. *Hydra* reproduces by budding. Ferns reproduce via haploid spore formation within sporangia.
