### Science

#### Q11. [Velocity-Time Graphs & Displacement] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Displacement is equal to the area under the velocity-time graph. Area of triangle $= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 4 = 4\text{ m}$.

#### Q12. [Plane Mirrors & Obstacles] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: An object at a larger distance from the mirror subtends a narrower angle past the visual obstruction and disappears first (Object R).

#### Q13. [Latent Heat of Fusion] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Ice at $0^\circ\text{C}$ absorbs latent heat of fusion to melt into water at $0^\circ\text{C}$. Temperature remains constant while thermal energy works to break intermolecular bonds.

#### Q14. [Refraction & Total Internal Reflection (TIR)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Ray bends towards the normal when passing from medium 1 to medium 2, indicating $\mu_2 > \mu_1$. Total internal reflection only occurs when entering an optically rarer medium ($"\sin i > \sin C"$), which is impossible at point R, meaning no TIR occurs at R.

#### Q15. [Kinematics & Acceleration Curves] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Ball rolling down an incline accelerates under gravity, so velocity increases continuously. Upon reaching the horizontal frictionless track, net horizontal force is zero, so velocity remains constant (horizontal line in Graph B).

#### Q16. [Electromagnets & Core Material] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: An electromagnet is an artificial temporary magnet created by winding an insulated copper wire coil around a soft iron core. It loses magnetism as soon as the electric current stops.

#### Q17. [Mirrors & Ray Optics] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Parallel incident rays converge after reflection from a concave mirror and diverge from a convex mirror. Only a flat plane mirror reflects parallel incident rays as a parallel beam.

#### Q18. [Distance vs Displacement] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Distance is the actual path length traversed, whereas displacement is the straight-line shortest vector from initial to final position.

#### Q19. [Magnetic Compass Needle Deflection] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: If a compass needle experiences zero net torque/deflection, the surrounding poles are symmetrically aligned.

#### Q20. [Structure of a Cyclone] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: A tropical cyclone acts as an atmospheric thermal engine fueled by latent heat of condensation. The central calm region of lowest barometric pressure is termed the 'eye'.

#### Q21. [Magnesium Oxide Reactions & Indicators] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $2\text{Mg} + \text{O}_2 \to 2\text{MgO}$. Dissolved in water: $\text{MgO} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{Mg(OH)}_2$. Magnesium hydroxide is basic, turning red litmus blue and phenolphthalein pink.

#### Q22. [Methods of Separation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Centrifugation separates blood cells from plasma; evaporation crystallizes dissolved salt; magnetic separation extracts iron filings; a separating funnel separates immiscible liquids (petrol and water).

#### Q23. [Acid-Base Indicator Tests] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: China rose indicator turns dark pink in acids (orange juice, vinegar), green in bases (slaked lime $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$), and shows no colour change in neutral solutions (glucose).

#### Q24. [Everyday Materials Classification] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Honey and mustard oil are edible lipids/carbohydrates; copper and gold are elemental transition metals.

#### Q25. [Reversibility of Changes] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Handpicking stones from rice is physical and reversible. Curdling of milk is chemical and irreversible. Breaking a flower vase is physical and irreversible.

#### Q26. [Non-Metallic Oxides & Acidity] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $\text{SO}_2$ is a non-metallic oxide. When dissolved in water, it forms sulphurous acid: $\text{SO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{H}_2\text{SO}_3$. Acids turn China rose indicator dark pink (magenta).

#### Q27. [Gas Evolution & Displacement Reactions] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Reaction of vinegar with baking soda releases $\text{CO}_2$ gas: $\text{CH}_3\text{COOH} + \text{NaHCO}_3 \to \text{CH}_3\text{COONa} + \text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{CO}_2\uparrow$. Iron in copper sulphate undergoes single displacement: $\text{Fe} + \text{CuSO}_4 \to \text{FeSO}_4 + \text{Cu}\downarrow$ (blue solution turns green; brown copper deposits on nail).

#### Q28. [Household Chemicals & Neutralisation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Detergents are basic (turn red litmus blue). Aerated soft drinks contain carbonic acid (turn China rose magenta). Neutralisation reactions are universally exothermic (release heat). Window cleaners contain ammonium hydroxide $\text{NH}_4\text{OH}$ (turn phenolphthalein pink).

#### Q29. [Animal Tissues (Cartilage)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Cartilage is a firm, flexible connective tissue found in joints, the ear pinna, and the tip of the nose that cushions bone ends.

#### Q30. [Hydrological Water Cycle Processes] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: P: evaporation, Q: condensation, R: infiltration, S: precipitation, T: transpiration. Excess precipitation causes flooding; prolonged absence of precipitation causes severe drought.

#### Q31. [Ciliates (Paramecium)] (1 Mark)

#### Q37. [Composting & Waste Sorting] (1 Mark)

#### Q38. [Phloem Transport & Haematology] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Phloem translocates photosynthetic carbohydrates (sucrose) bidirectional throughout plants. Anaemia is characterized by insufficient hemoglobin or red blood cells. Normal blood contains roughly $5 \times 10^6$ RBCs per $\text{mm}^3$, vastly outnumbering WBCs ($6000-8000$ per $\text{mm}^3$).

#### Q39. [Plant Gaseous Exchange Organs] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**

#### Q40. [Photosynthesis & Nutrition Stages] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Chlorophyll traps solar radiation for photosynthesis. Mushrooms are heterotrophic saprophytic fungi. Ingestion is the intake of food; assimilation is incorporating absorbed nutrients into bodily tissues for growth and respiration.

#### Q41. [High-Altitude Adaptations (Yak)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Yaks have long thick fleece for thermal insulation, large hearts and lung capacities with high red blood cell counts to survive hypoxia, and broad cloven hooves to traverse snow.

#### Q42. [Advantages of Vegetative Propagation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Vegetative reproduction produces genetically identical clones, bypasses the vulnerable seedling phase for faster maturity, and propagates seedless varieties (banana, sugarcane).

#### Q43. [Forest Economic Products] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Forests yield renewable economic timber, pharmaceutical herbal compounds, commercial resins, tannins, latex, and gums.

#### Q44. [Desert Adaptations (Kangaroo Rat)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: The kangaroo rat survives in arid deserts without drinking water by oxidizing dry seeds into metabolic water and concentrating urine via ultra-long loops of Henle.
