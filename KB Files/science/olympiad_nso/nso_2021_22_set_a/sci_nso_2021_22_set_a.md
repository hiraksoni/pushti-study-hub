### Science

#### Q11. [Optics (Multiple Reflections)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Parallel plane mirrors $M_1$ and $M_2$ at distance $25\text{ cm}$. Successive images form at $5\text{ cm}, 25\text{ cm}, 35\text{ cm}$ behind mirrors.

#### Q12. [Uniform Motion] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: An aeroplane cruising at a steady speed of $600\text{ km/h}$ in a straight line undergoes uniform motion.

#### Q13. [Concave Lens Properties] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: A concave lens is thinner at the centre than at the edges and diverges parallel incident light rays.

#### Q14. [Magnetic Materials] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Iron, nickel, and steel are ferromagnetic and attracted by magnets. Copper, silver, and gold are non-magnetic.

#### Q15. [Speed and Relative Motion] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: In equal time, X covers $50\text{ m}$ while Y covers $60\text{ m} \implies$ Speed ratio $= 5 : 6$.

#### Q16. [Radiation & Surface Absorption] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Polished shiny surfaces are poor emitters and good reflectors of radiant heat; dull black surfaces emit and absorb heat rapidly.

#### Q17. [Aerofoil Lift (Bernoulli)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Curved upper wing surface forces air to move faster, creating low pressure on top and high pressure below, producing aerodynamic lift.

#### Q18. [Parallel Electric Circuits] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Adding bulb 4 in parallel lowers circuit equivalent resistance, increasing total current and brightness of bulb 3.

#### Q19. [Heating Curves & States of Matter] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: During constant-temperature phase change (process 2), solid melts to liquid. Point 4 represents coexistence of liquid and boiling vapour.

#### Q20. [Magnetic Induction] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Soft iron pieces placed near a magnet undergo magnetic induction; opposite poles develop at adjacent ends and attract.

#### Q21. [Turmeric & China Rose Indicators] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Turmeric turns red in basic solutions (limewater Y). China rose turns magenta in acids (lemon juice Z) and green in bases. Salt solution X is neutral.

#### Q22. [Properties of Common Substances] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Glycerine is miscible and edible; table salt is soluble; copper coin is lustrous; matchstick floats on water.

#### Q23. [Chemical vs Physical Changes] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Decaying fallen leaves is chemical (bacterial decomposition). Alloying is metallurgical. Paper shredding and crystallization are physical.

#### Q24. [Sublimation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Camphor, naphthalene, and iodine sublime directly from solid to vapour upon heating; table salt and sugar do not.

#### Q25. [Corrosion & Rusting Conditions] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Rusting requires both oxygen and moisture. The waterline region R of a ship exposed to splashing aerated seawater rusts fastest.

#### Q26. [Water of Crystallisation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Blue vitriol $\text{CuSO}_4 \cdot 5\text{H}_2\text{O}$ turns white anhydrous $\text{CuSO}_4$ upon heating, releasing water vapour Z.

#### Q27. [Methyl Orange Indicator] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Methyl orange turns pink/red in acids (tamarind pulp, dilute acid mixture) and yellow in bases (glass cleaner).

#### Q28. [Chemical Equations] (1 Mark)

#### Q29. [Digestive System Physiology] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Salivary amylase breaks starch into maltose in buccal cavity. Bile from liver is stored in gallbladder. Large intestine absorbs water/salts.

#### Q30. [Indian Wool Breeds] (1 Mark)

#### Q31. [Alimentary Canal Dimensions] (1 Mark)

#### Q32. [Polar Bear Insulation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Polar bears possess two thick layers of dense hollow hair fur over a thick layer of subcutaneous fat (blubber) for extreme thermal insulation.

#### Q33. [Trophic Levels & Food Webs] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: P and V are autotrophic green producers; W and S are carnivores; U and R are omnivores; T is a saprotrophic decomposer.

#### Q34. [Circulatory Anatomy (Heart Vessels)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Aorta carries oxygenated blood from left ventricle to body. Vena cava returns deoxygenated blood to right atrium. Pulmonary artery carries blood to lungs.

#### Q35. [Mechanism of Inhalation] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: During inhalation, diaphragm contracts and flattens, external intercostals pull ribs upward and outward, thoracic volume expands, and air rushes in.

#### Q36. [Anemophily (Wind Pollination)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Wind-pollinated flowers are inconspicuous, small, light-coloured, lack fragrance/nectar, and produce enormous quantities of dry airborne pollen.

#### Q37. [Sources of Nutrients] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Human food is sourced from autotrophic plants and heterotrophic domesticated animals across diverse cultural culinary traditions.

#### Q38. [Nutritional Deficiencies] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Deficiency of Vitamin C causes scurvy (bleeding gums). Proteins are essential for muscle repair. Iron deficiency causes nutritional anaemia.

#### Q39. [Combustion of Animal Fibres] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Proteinaceous animal fibres (silk, wool) curl away from flame, burn slowly with odor of burning hair, and self-extinguish; plant cellulose burns easily.

#### Q40. [Vegetative Adaptations] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Radish and carrot are storage tap roots; grapevine has tendrils; *Opuntia* has flattened photosynthetic phylloclade stems.

#### Q41. [Desert Adaptations (Camel)] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Camels conserve water by excreting thick concentrated urine, producing dry dung, withstanding high body temperature swings without sweating.

#### Q42. [Silk Moth Life Cycle] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Stages: Egg $\to$ Silkworm larva $\to$ Pupa enclosed in cocoon $\to$ Adult silkmoth.

#### Q43. [Atmospheric Pressure & Altitude] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Atmospheric air density and barometric pressure decrease exponentially with increasing altitude.

#### Q44. [Human Skeletal Structure] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Human vertebral column contains 33 vertebrae (7 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar, 5 sacral, 4 coccygeal). Rib cage has 12 pairs (24 total ribs).

#### Q45. [Complete vs Incomplete Flowers] (1 Mark)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Complete flowers (Petunia, Hibiscus, mustard) contain all 4 whorls. Incomplete/unisexual flowers (papaya, date palm) lack stamens or carpels.


### Achievers Section

#### Q46. [Electromagnetic Induction & Thumb Rule] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(B)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Right-hand thumb rule determines circular magnetic field lines around conductor. Current upwards produces anticlockwise circular field lines.

#### Q47. [Hydrolysis of Salts & pH] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: $(\text{NH}_4)_2\text{SO}_4$ (weak base $+$ strong acid) has $\text{pH} < 7$ (acidic). $\text{CuSO}_4$ has $\text{pH} < 7$ (acidic). $\text{NaCl}$ (strong acid $+$ strong base) has $\text{pH} = 7$ (neutral). Washing soda $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$ (strong base $+$ weak acid) has $\text{pH} > 7$ (basic).

#### Q48. [Nutrition Modes (Mushroom vs Coleus)] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(A)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Mushroom is a saprotrophic fungus lacking chlorophyll; *Coleus* is a photosynthetic autotrophic angiosperm.

#### Q49. [Sewage Treatment Stages] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(D)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Bar screens trap coarse trash $\to$ grit chamber settles dense sand/gravel $\to$ primary clarifier sinks sludge $\to$ aeration tank decomposes dissolved organic matter with aerobic bacteria.

#### Q50. [Spirometry Pulmonary Volume Curve] (3 Marks)
- **Correct Option**: **(C)**
- **Comprehensive Solution**: Curve section P-to-R represents exhalation (thoracic volume decreases, diaphragm relaxes and arches upward). R-to-T represents inhalation (diaphragm contracts and flattens down).
