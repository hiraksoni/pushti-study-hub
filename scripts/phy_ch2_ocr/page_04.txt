However, the air between you and the fire is a bad conductor of heat and does not get heated up much. Therefore, heat is not reaching you by conduction. It also Cannot reach you by convection since convection currents rise upwards. Therefore there is another Iryethod by which heat energy reaches you. This method is called radiation of heat. Radiation is the only process of heat transfer that can occur in the absence of any form of medium. It refers to the transmission of energy as electromagnetic radiation. Radiation is the fastest mode of transfer Of heat. All hot objects—solid, liquid or gas emit radiant energy. When radiant energy falls on another Object, it is partially absorbed and partially reflected. The amount of heat absorbed or radiated only depends upon the nature and the colour Of the Object. Dark colours are good absorbers of radiant heat, while light colours are poor absorbers and good reflectors Of heat. Therefore every hot object emits (gives out) invisible heat rays in all directions heat can from a hot body to a cold body by radiation even if there is no rnaterial medium (like solid, liquid or gas) between them. Heat from the Sun reaches the Earth by the process Of radiation. In this case. conduction or convection is not possible because Of the vacuum between the Sun and the Earth.
