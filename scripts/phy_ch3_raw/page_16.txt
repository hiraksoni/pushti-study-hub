14. The Rajdhani express travels at a speed
of 80 km/h. The distance between Delhi and
Chennai is 2200 km. If it starts from Delhi
at 3.00 p.m., then at what time does it reach
Chennai?
Ans.:Speed - 80 km/h; Distance = 2200 km.
It covers 80 km in one hour. Therefore, it covers
2200 km in (2200 + 80) = 27.5 hours.
It reaches Chennai next day at 6.30 p.m.
