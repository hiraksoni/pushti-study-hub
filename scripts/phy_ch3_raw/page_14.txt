6.
Can the displacement be greater than the
distance travelled by an object? Give reason.
Ans.: No, the displacement of an object can be
either equal to or less than the distance travelled
by the object, because the displacement is the
shortest distance between initial and final
positions of the object while distance travelled
is the length of the actual path traversed by the
object.
