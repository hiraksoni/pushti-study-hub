Board Alignment: CBSE, ICSE, and State Boards Equivalence

Is the NCERT Curriculum common across all boards? YES. Under the National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE), fundamental concepts and learning competencies are unified across India:

CBSE Board Directly adopts NCERT textbooks (Ganita Prakash, Curiosity, Exploring Society, Poorvi, Malhar). 100% of school examinations are built from these core chapters.
State Boards (SCERTs) Over 90% of Indian state boards (Maharashtra, Gujarat, UP, Karnataka, etc.) have adopted NCERT syllabus verbatim or with regional language translations. The scope and chapter order match 1:1.
ICSE Board (CISCE) Uses private publishers (such as Ref 1, Ref 4, Ref 3). The conceptual core matches NCERT, but ICSE maintains topics like Pythagoras Theorem, Recurring Decimals, and HCF/LCM directly in Grade 7.
