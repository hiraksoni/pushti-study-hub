17.1 Markets & Their Types Wholesale vs Retail

A market is a place where buyers and sellers go to exchange goods[cite: 5].
• Wholesale Markets: Goods are bought and sold in bulk to other traders[cite: 5].
• Retail Markets: Goods are sold directly to final consumers in smaller quantities[cite: 5].

Types of Retail Markets Weekly to Online

🎪 Weekly Markets

  • Held once a week on specific days with temporary stalls[cite: 5].
  • No permanent building/rent overheads $\rightarrow$ lower prices[cite: 5]!

🏪 Neighbourhood Shops & Malls

  • Neighbourhood Shops: Provide daily necessities near home, often on credit[cite: 5].
  • Shopping Malls & Branded Stores: Multi-storeyed shops selling high-priced branded goods[cite: 5].

💻 Online Retailers

  • Virtual stores operating with lower overhead costs directly to doorstep[cite: 5].
17.2 The Chain of Markets Producer to Consumer

🌾 1. Producers

  • Grow or manufacture goods (farmers or factory owners)[cite: 5].

🚚 2. Traders (Wholesale & Retail)

  • Move goods along the chain, adding transport, rent, and profit margins[cite: 5].

🛒 3. Consumers

  • Buy goods for personal or family use at final retail prices[cite: 5].
17.3 & 17.4 Cooperatives & Mindful Buying Fair Trade

🚜 Cooperatives

  • Organizations formed voluntarily by producers/farmers to sell directly and eliminate middlemen[cite: 5].

🛍️ Mindful Buying

  • Making conscious purchasing decisions based on actual needs, supporting local producers and sustainable environments[cite: 5].
Master Multiple Choice Questions
1. Which of the following markets happens once a week?[cite: 5]
Explanation: Weekly markets take place once a week on specific days[cite: 5].
2. Which of the following markets sells goods from only one brand?[cite: 5]
Explanation: Branded stores sell products from a single specific brand[cite: 5].
3. Which of the following is an organisation formed by people with shared interests and goals?[cite: 5]
Explanation: Cooperatives are formed by members with shared economic goals[cite: 5].
Confusing Two-Choice Drills
1. Online retailers have ________ overhead costs compared to physical shops.[cite: 5]
Why confusion happens: Online stores do not maintain physical showroom spaces, giving them lower overhead costs[cite: 5].
Interactive True / False Arena
1. In the chain of markets, the price of a product increases at every step.[cite: 5]
Reason: TRUE. Intermediaries add expenses and profit margins at every step[cite: 5].
