Chronological Overview of the Four Empires
320 CE – 550 CE
1. The Gupta Empire (Capitals: Pataliputra, Ujjain)

Key Rulers: Chandragupta I (founded with Lichchhavi alliance), Samudragupta (expanded across Aryavarta & South India), Chandragupta II 'Vikramaditya'. Ended due to Huna attacks.

543 CE – 753 CE
2. The Chalukyas of Badami (Capital: Badami)

Key Rulers: Pulakesin I (founder), Pulakesin II (greatest ruler; stopped Harsha at Narmada River). Known for rock-cut cave temples at Badami and Pattadakal.

575 CE – 668 CE+
3. The Pallavas of Kanchipuram (Capital: Kanchipuram)

Key Rulers: Simhavishnu (independent founder), Mahendravarman I, Narasimhavarman I 'Mamalla' (built Mamallapuram, defeated Pulakesin II).

606 CE – 647 CE
4. Harshavardhana's Empire (Capitals: Thaneshwar, Kanauj)

Key Rulers: Harshavardhana (Pushyabhuti family, took throne at age 16). Patron of Nalanda; wrote Ratnavali & Nagananda.

Historical Sources

Literary Puranas, plays (*Ratnavali*, *Devichandraguptam*), and foreign accounts by Chinese pilgrims Xuanzang and Fa Xian.

Archaeological Gold coins (Guptas), rock-cut monuments, and Prashastis (eulogy inscriptions in praise of rulers like Prayagraj Prashasti by Harisena).

Gupta Administrative Hierarchy
  • Emperor: Supreme head, advised by Mantris.
  • Bhuktis (Provinces): Governed by Uparikas.
  • Vishyas (Districts): Governed by Vishyapatis.
  • Gramas (Villages): Managed by Gramikas.
  • Samantas: Military chiefs paid in land grants.

Cultural & Scientific Achievements
KingdomKey Contributors & Achievements
Guptas"Golden Age" — Astronomer Aryabhata, Poet Kalidasa (*Shakuntala*), Writer Vishakhadatta. Center of learning: Nalanda University.
HarshaPlays written by Harsha: *Ratnavali*, *Nagananda*. Court poet Banabhatta wrote *Harshacharita* and *Kadambari*.
Chalukyas & PallavasChalukya rock-cut temples at Badami & Pattadakal. Pallava *Shore Temple* and *Pancha Pandava Rathas* at Mamallapuram.
Everyday Life of the People
Economy & Tax
  • Main occupation: Farming and animal rearing.
  • Main source of revenue: Land taxes.
  • Trade conducted with Rome, Southeast Asia, and Africa.
Society & Religion
  • Rigid caste system determined occupations.
  • Religious tolerance: Hinduism, Buddhism, and Jainism coexisted peacefully.
Textbook Exercises — Interactive Q&A (Click to Reveal Answers)
Q1. Who captured nine kingdoms in Aryavarta? (MCQ)
Answer: Samudragupta[cite: 2].
Q2. What was the capital city of Harshavardhana? (MCQ)
Answer: Kanauj (shifted from Thaneshwar)[cite: 1, 2].
Q3. Which Chalukya ruler defeated Harshavardhana at the Narmada River? (MCQ)
Answer: Pulakesin II[cite: 1, 2].
Q4. What were the governors of provinces (Bhuktis) called in the Gupta Empire? (MCQ)
Answer: Uparikas[cite: 1, 2].
Q5. Who wrote *Harshacharita* in Harsha's court? (MCQ)
Answer: Banabhatta[cite: 1, 2].
Q6. True/False: Chandragupta II took the title of Maharajadhiraja.
Answer: False. Chandragupta II took the title of Vikramaditya[cite: 1, 2]. (Chandragupta I and Samudragupta took the title Maharajadhiraja)[cite: 2].
Q7. What was a 'Prashasti' and what was its main limitation?
Answer: A prashasti is a Sanskrit poem composed by court poets in praise of a king[cite: 2]. Its main limitation is that it only describes the victories and good qualities of rulers, ignoring their failures[cite: 1, 2].
Q8. Arrange chronologically: Harsha, Guptas, Chalukyas, Pallavas.
Answer: The Gupta Empire (320 CE) → Chalukyas (543 CE) → Pallavas (575 CE) → Harsha's Empire (606 CE)[cite: 1, 2].
