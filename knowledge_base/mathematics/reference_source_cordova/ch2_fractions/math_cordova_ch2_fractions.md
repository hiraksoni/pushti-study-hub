## 1. Chapter Overview & Core Theoretical Principles

> **Syllabus Scope**: Cordova Core Chapter 2 covering multiplication of fractions, reciprocal/multiplicative inverse, division of fractions by whole numbers and fractions, and real-life problem modeling.

### Multiplication & Division of Fractions
- Multiplication: $\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ac}{bd}$.
- Division: $\frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c}$.

### Reciprocal (Multiplicative Inverse)
Reciprocal of $\frac{a}{b}$ is $\frac{b}{a}$. Note: $0$ has no reciprocal.

#### [CORD_MATH2_MCQ_003] Evaluate: $\frac{5}{9} \div \frac{15}{27}$.

  - 5/9
  - 25/81
  - 3
- **Answer**: **1**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: Multiply by reciprocal: $\frac{5}{9} \times \frac{27}{15} = \frac{5}{15} \times \frac{27}{9} = \frac{1}{3} \times 3 = 1$.

#### [CORD_MATH2_MCQ_004] Which is greater: $\frac{2}{3}\text{ of } \frac{3}{4}$ or $\frac{3}{5}\text{ of } \frac{5}{8}$?
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Fraction Comparison` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
  - 2/3 of 3/4
  - 3/5 of 5/8
  - Both are equal
  - Cannot be determined
- **Answer**: **2/3 of 3/4**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2} = 0.5$. $\frac{3}{5} \times \frac{5}{8} = \frac{3}{8} = 0.375$. Since $0.5 > 0.375$, $\frac{2}{3}\text{ of } \frac{3}{4}$ is greater.

- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Proper Fraction Products` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`

### 2.4 Subjective, Step-by-Step Solved & Analytical Problems (2 Items)

#### [CORD_MATH2_SA_001] A car runs $16\text{ km}$ using 1 liter of petrol. How much distance will it cover using $2\frac{3}{4}\text{ liters}$ of petrol?
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `3` | **Topic**: `Fraction Word Problem` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
- **Step-by-Step Model Solution**:
  1. Convert mixed fraction: $2\frac{3}{4} = \frac{11}{4}\text{ liters}$.
2. Distance covered $= 16 \times \frac{11}{4}$.
3. Simplify: $4 \times 11 = \mathbf{44\text{ km}}$.
- **Marking Rubric & Working Notes**: Fractional scaling applied to distance and fuel rate.

#### [CORD_MATH2_LA_001] In a school of 1200 students, $\frac{5}{8}$ of the students are boys. Among the boys, $\frac{1}{5}$ participate in cricket and $\frac{2}{5}$ in football. Among the girls, $\frac{2}{3}$ participate in sports.
(a) How many girls are in the school?
(b) How many boys do NOT play cricket or football?
(c) What is the total number of students participating in sports?
- **Difficulty**: `hard_hots` | **Marks**: `5` | **Topic**: `Multi-Step Fraction Problem` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
- **Step-by-Step Model Solution**:
  • **(a) Number of Girls**:
  - Boys $= \frac{5}{8} \times 1200 = 5 \times 150 = 750$.
  - Girls $= 1200 - 750 = \mathbf{450}$.
• **(b) Boys not playing cricket or football**:
  - Cricket boys $= \frac{1}{5} \times 750 = 150$.
  - Football boys $= \frac{2}{5} \times 750 = 300$.
  - Boys playing either $= 150 + 300 = 450$.
  - Boys not playing $= 750 - 450 = \mathbf{300}$.
• **(c) Total Sports Participation**:
  - Sports girls $= \frac{2}{3} \times 450 = 300$.
  - Sports boys $= 450$.
  - Total sports students $= 450 + 300 = \mathbf{750\text{ students}}$.
- **Marking Rubric & Working Notes**: Multi-tier fractional division of student populations.
