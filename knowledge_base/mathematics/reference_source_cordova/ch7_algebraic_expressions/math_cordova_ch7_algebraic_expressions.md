## 1. Chapter Overview & Core Theoretical Principles

> **Syllabus Scope**: Cordova Core Chapter 7 covering degrees of algebraic expressions, monomials, binomials, trinomials, polynomials, factor trees, column addition, and subtraction laws.

### Classification of Polynomials
- Monomial: 1 term ($7x^2$).
- Binomial: 2 terms ($2x + 3y$).
- Trinomial: 3 terms ($x^2 - 4x + 7$).

### Degree of a Polynomial
Highest power of variable in one variable ($5x^3 - 4x^2 + 7$ has degree 3). Highest sum of exponents in multivariable term ($3x^2y^3$ has degree 5).

### Subtraction of Polynomials
Change the sign of every term in the subtrahend and add to the minuend: $-(a - b) = -a + b$.

#### [CORD_MATH7_MCQ_003] Simplify: $12a^2b - 7ab^2 + 5a^2b - 3ab^2$.
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Addition of Like Terms` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`

  - 7ab
- **Answer**: **17a^2b - 10ab^2**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: Group like terms: $(12 + 5)a^2b + (-7 - 3)ab^2 = 17a^2b - 10ab^2$.

#### [CORD_MATH7_MCQ_004] If $x = 2$ and $y = -1$, find the value of $3x^2 - 2xy + y^2$.

  - 13
  - 9
  - 15
- **Answer**: **17**
- **Mathematical Rationale & Step-by-Step Solution**: $3(2)^2 - 2(2)(-1) + (-1)^2 = 3(4) - (-4) + 1 = 12 + 4 + 1 = 17$.

### 2.3 True or False (2 Items)

#### [CORD_MATH7_TF_001] $4xy^2$ and $-5x^2y$ are like terms. (True/False)
- **Difficulty**: `easy` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Like Terms` | **Source**: `textbook_exercise`
- **Answer**: **False**
- **Justification & Counter-Example**: Like terms must have identical variables with identical powers. In $4xy^2$ the power of $y$ is 2, whereas in $-5x^2y$ the power of $x$ is 2.

#### [CORD_MATH7_TF_002] Subtracting $3x - 4y$ from $7x + 2y$ yields $4x + 6y$. (True/False)
- **Difficulty**: `easy` | **Marks**: `1` | **Topic**: `Subtraction Law` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
- **Answer**: **True**
- **Justification & Counter-Example**: $(7x + 2y) - (3x - 4y) = 7x + 2y - 3x + 4y = 4x + 6y$.

### 2.4 Subjective, Step-by-Step Solved & Analytical Problems (2 Items)

#### [CORD_MATH7_SA_001] Subtract $4a - 7ab + 3b + 12$ from $12a - 9ab + 5b - 3$.
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `3` | **Topic**: `Column Subtraction` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
- **Step-by-Step Model Solution**:
  Arrange in column format and change the signs of the subtrahend:
$$\begin{array}{r@{\quad}l}
  12a - 9ab + 5b - 3 & \\
- (4a - 7ab + 3b + 12) & \\
\hline
  (12 - 4)a + (-9 + 7)ab + (5 - 3)b + (-3 - 12) & = \mathbf{8a - 2ab + 2b - 15}
\end{array}$$
- **Marking Rubric & Working Notes**: Standard column subtraction with sign reversal.

#### [CORD_MATH7_LA_001] The three sides of a triangle are represented by $(3x^2 - 4x + 5)$, $(2x^2 + 7x - 2)$, and $(4x^2 - 5x + 1)$.
(a) Find a simplified expression for the perimeter of the triangle.
(b) If $x = 3\text{ cm}$, calculate the numerical perimeter.
- **Difficulty**: `hard_hots` | **Marks**: `5` | **Topic**: `Perimeter & Polynomial Modeling` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
- **Step-by-Step Model Solution**:
  • **(a) Perimeter Expression**:
  $$\text{Perimeter} = (3x^2 - 4x + 5) + (2x^2 + 7x - 2) + (4x^2 - 5x + 1)$$
  Group like terms:
  $$= (3 + 2 + 4)x^2 + (-4 + 7 - 5)x + (5 - 2 + 1)$$
  $$= \mathbf{9x^2 - 2x + 4}$$
• **(b) Evaluation at $x = 3\text{ cm}$**:
  $$\text{Perimeter} = 9(3)^2 - 2(3) + 4 = 9(9) - 6 + 4 = 81 - 6 + 4 = \mathbf{79\text{ cm}}$$
- **Marking Rubric & Working Notes**: Geometric application of polynomial addition and numerical substitution.
