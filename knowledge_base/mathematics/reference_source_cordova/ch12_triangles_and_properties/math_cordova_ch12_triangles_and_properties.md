### Medians vs Altitudes
- **Median**: Connects vertex to midpoint of opposite side (always inside).
- **Altitude**: Perpendicular from vertex to opposite side (can be inside, on side, or outside).

### Pythagoras Theorem & Triplets
$c^2 = a^2 + b^2$. Common triplets: $(3, 4, 5)$, $(5, 12, 13)$, $(6, 8, 10)$, $(8, 15, 17)$.

#### [CORD_MATH12_SA_001] A ladder $15\text{ m}$ long reaches a window $12\text{ m}$ high from the ground on placing it against a wall at a distance $a$. Find the distance of the foot of the ladder from the wall.
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `3` | **Topic**: `Ladder & Wall Pythagoras Problem` | **Source**: `chapter_notes`
- **Step-by-Step Model Solution**:
  1. Let wall height $h = 12\text{ m}$, ladder length (hypotenuse) $L = 15\text{ m}$, base distance $= a$.
2. By Pythagoras theorem in right-angled $\triangle$:
   $$a^2 + h^2 = L^2$$
   $$a^2 + 12^2 = 15^2$$
   $$a^2 + 144 = 225$$
   $$a^2 = 225 - 144 = 81$$
   $$a = \sqrt{81} = \mathbf{9\text{ m}}$$.
The foot of the ladder is $9\text{ m}$ from the wall.
