## 1. Chapter Overview & Core Theoretical Principles

> **Syllabus Scope**: Exploration of large numbers across Indian and International place value systems, powers of ten, estimation strategies, commas placement, and real-life large measurement scales.

### Indian vs International Place Value Systems
- **Indian System**: Periods grouped as Ones, Thousands, Lakhs, Crores. Commas: 3 digits from right, then every 2 digits (e.g., $1,23,45,678$).
- **International System**: Periods grouped as Ones, Thousands, Millions, Billions. Commas: every 3 digits from right (e.g., $12,345,678$).
- **Equivalence**: $1\text{ million} = 10\text{ lakhs} = 10^6$; $10\text{ millions} = 1\text{ crore} = 10^7$; $1\text{ billion} = 100\text{ crores} = 10^9$.

### Powers of Ten & Magnitude
Powers of ten express scale compactly: $10^1 = 10$, $10^2 = 100$, $10^3 = 1,000$ (thousand), $10^5 = 1,00,000$ (lakh), $10^6 = 1,000,000$ (million), $10^7 = 1,00,00,000$ (crore).

### Estimation & Rounding Principles
To round off to a target place: inspect the next digit to the right. If $< 5$, round down; if $\ge 5$, round up by adding 1 to the target place value.

### 2.4 Subjective, Step-by-Step Solved & Analytical Problems (2 Items)

#### [MTG_MATH1_SA_001] The distance between the Earth and the Moon is approximately $3,84,400\text{ km}$. Express this number in the International system of numeration and in expanded form using powers of 10.
- **Difficulty**: `medium` | **Marks**: `2` | **Topic**: `Distance in Real Life` | **Source**: `textbook_exercise`
- **Step-by-Step Model Solution**:
  1. International Numeration: $384,400\text{ km}$ (Three hundred eighty-four thousand four hundred kilometers).
2. Expanded form: $3 \times 10^5 + 8 \times 10^4 + 4 \times 10^3 + 4 \times 10^2$.
- **Marking Rubric & Working Notes**: Accurate period grouping and power-of-ten decomposition.

#### [MTG_MATH1_SA_002] Estimate the product $578 \times 161$ by rounding each number to its greatest place.
- **Difficulty**: `hard` | **Marks**: `3` | **Topic**: `Estimation of Operations` | **Source**: `textbook_exercise`
- **Step-by-Step Model Solution**:
  1. 578 rounds up to 600 (hundreds place, tens digit is 7).
2. 161 rounds up to 200 (hundreds place, tens digit is 6).
3. Estimated Product: $600 \times 200 = 120,000$.
- **Marking Rubric & Working Notes**: Standard general rounding rule for estimation of products.
