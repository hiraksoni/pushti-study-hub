## 1. Chapter Overview & Core Theoretical Principles

> **Syllabus Scope**: Fractions as parts of wholes, area models, proper, improper, and mixed fractions, ancient Indian algorithms for fraction operations (Brahmagupta's methods), and comparing fractions.

### Fraction Representation & Area Models
A fraction $\frac{a}{b}$ represents $a$ equal parts out of a total of $b$ parts of a whole.

### Brahmagupta's Fraction Algorithms
In the *Brāhmasphuṭasiddhānta* (628 CE), Brahmagupta codified:
- Addition/Subtraction: $\frac{a}{b} \pm \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ad \pm bc}{bd}$.
- Multiplication: $\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ac}{bd}$.
