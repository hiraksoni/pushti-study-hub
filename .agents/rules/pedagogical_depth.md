# The Exhaustive "Why / How / When" Pedagogical Depth Protocol (Zero-Superficiality Standard)

This rule governs all content creation, UI authoring, theory explanation, interactive simulators, and question solutions across all subjects (Social Science, Science, Mathematics, ICT, Languages) in Pushti Study Hub.

---

## 1. The Core Philosophy: "A Child Must NEVER Need to Open the Textbook"

A study portal that replaces textbooks cannot merely provide high-level bullet points or 1-2 sentence gloss-overs. 
When a 12-year-old student (Pushti) asks:
- *"Why does this happen?"*
- *"How does this transformation actually work step-by-step?"*
- *"When and over what timeframe does this occur?"*
- *"Why does this rule or phenomenon apply here but not there?"*

The portal must **already answer these questions in full scientific and historical depth** right inside the module.

---

## 2. The Mandatory 5 Dimensions for Every Concept & Simulator Stage

Whenever an AI model, script, or developer authors a concept breakdown, stage in an interactive simulator, theory pillar, or comprehensive answer, it **MUST explicitly cover all 5 dimensions**:

### 1. ❓ WHY? (Underlying Scientific, Historical, or Civic Drivers)
- What fundamental forces, laws of physics/chemistry, economic pressures, or historical motivations cause this phenomenon to occur?
- *Examples*:
  - *Why do rocks melt into magma?* (Radioactive decay heat and primordial gravitational accretion heat in the mantle exceed 1,200°C).
  - *Why did Earth differentiate into concentric shells?* (4.6 billion years ago, gravitational density differentiation caused heavy Fe/Ni to sink while light silicates floated).
  - *Why do sedimentary rocks form layers?* (Episodic, seasonal deposition of sediments over millions of years creates distinct strata).
  - *Why did Ashoka embrace Dhamma?* (Grief and remorse over the slaughter of 100,000 soldiers in the Kalinga War).

### 2. ⚙️ HOW? (Step-by-Step Mechanical, Chemical, or Institutional Process)
- Walk through the exact sequential mechanism without skipping steps.
- *Examples*:
  - *How does loose sand turn into hard rock (Lithification)?* 
    1. **Weathering & Erosion**: Mechanical/chemical fracturing into sand grains.
    2. **Deposition**: Water currents slow down in lakes/seas, dropping sediment in horizontal beds.
    3. **Compaction**: Weight of overlying mud/water squeezes out interstitial water, reducing pore space.
    4. **Cementation**: Mineral solutions (dissolved silica $SiO_2$, calcite $CaCO_3$, or iron oxide) percolate through pores and precipitate, acting as natural chemical glue that binds grains into rock.

### 3. ⏳ WHEN & TIMEFRAME? (Temporal Scales & Geological Sequence)
- Explicitly state the time dimension:
  - Is it instantaneous (volcanic explosion, earthquake fault rupture)?
  - Is it seasonal (annual flood river deposits, meander breaches)?
  - Does it take millions or billions of years (continental plate drift at ~2 cm/year; 4.6-billion-year nebula genesis; 66-million-year-old Deccan Traps)?

### 4. 🔬 WHAT? (Complete Scientific Taxonomy, Genetic Sub-classes & Mineralogy)
- Never mention just a single generic term. Always provide the **complete scientific classification**:
  - **Sedimentary Rocks Taxonomy**:
    1. *Mechanically formed (Clastic)*: Sandstone, Shale, Conglomerate, Mudstone, Loess.
    2. *Organically formed (Biogenic)*: Limestone (from marine shells/corals), Coal (from compressed swamp peat bogs).
    3. *Chemically formed (Evaporites)*: Rock salt (Halite), Gypsum, Potash.
  - **Igneous Rocks Taxonomy**:
    1. *Intrusive (Plutonic)*: Granite, Diorite, Gabbro (slow subterranean cooling $\to$ large interlocking crystals).
    2. *Extrusive (Volcanic)*: Basalt, Andesite, Obsidian, Pumice (rapid surface cooling $\to$ fine crystals or glassy texture).

### 5. 🧬 CRITICAL PECULIARITIES & EXAM TRAPS (The "Why Only Here?" Dimension)
- Address the deepest conceptual questions and common student pitfalls:
  - *Why do ONLY sedimentary rocks contain fossils?* (Intense volcanic magma heat in igneous rocks incinerates organic matter; crushing tectonic pressure/heat in metamorphic rocks obliterates fossils. Only sedimentary strata gently bury and preserve remains in oxygen-depleted anaerobic conditions).
  - *Why is the Inner Core solid despite temperatures of 6,000°C?* (Hydrostatic overburden pressure exceeding 3.6 million atmospheres locks iron-nickel atoms into a rigid solid crystalline lattice, preventing melting).
  - *Why can S-waves not travel through the Outer Core?* (S-waves are transverse shear waves that require shear rigidity, which liquid fluids cannot sustain; this proved to seismologists that the outer core is liquid).
  - *Why are desert mushroom rocks narrow at the bottom?* (Saltation zone: heavy sand grains bounce within 1 to 2 meters of the ground, causing ferocious low-altitude abrasion, while upper air carries only fine, non-abrasive dust).

## 3. The Anti-Superficiality Law for Knowledge Bases and Builders

1. **NO Discarding Rich KB Content**: If a Knowledge Base file (`.md` or `.json`) contains deep theoretical modules, chemical percentages, geological gradients, or historical case studies, **they MUST be fully reflected in the user interface**.
2. **NO 1-Sentence Summaries**: A simulator stage, interactive card, or conceptual pillar must NEVER consist of just 1-2 generic sentences. Every card must contain:
   - Process Title & Badge
   - Comprehensive Explanatory Text (covering Why, How, When)
   - Complete Classification / Taxonomy Pills
   - Real-World Indian & Global Anchors (e.g. Red Fort, Taj Mahal, Deccan Traps, Barren Island, Bhuj, Sundarbans)
   - Deep Pedagogical Inquiry (addressing common student traps).
