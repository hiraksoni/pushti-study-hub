Chapter 1: Nouns: Formation

  • Nouns are the names of persons, places, things or animals.
  • Proper Nouns: Unique names of specific persons, places or things. Proper nouns always start with a capital letter. (e.g. Albert Einstein, New York, Titanic)
  • Common Nouns: General terms used for any persons, places or things. They are not specific and are only capitalised at the beginning of a sentence. (e.g. teacher, city, book)
  • Collective Nouns: Represent collection of individuals, animals or items viewed as one entity. (e.g. a team of players, a flock of birds, a bunch of grapes, a class of students)
  • Abstract Nouns: Denote ideas, qualities or conditions that are intangible and cannot be perceived with the senses. These nouns often symbolise emotions or concepts. (e.g. love, freedom, bravery, wisdom)
  • Common Suffixes to form Abstract Nouns:
    • From Adjectives: deep -> depth, strong -> strength, true -> truth, popular -> popularity
    • From Verbs: judge -> judgement, act -> action, know -> knowledge, move -> movement
    • From Common Nouns: friend -> friendship, child -> childhood, slave -> slavery, beauty -> beautiful (Wait, beautiful is an adjective, beauty is the abstract noun)
  • Concrete Nouns: Nouns that refer to the things we can touch, see, hear, smell or taste. (e.g. trees, pencils, books, mugs, wood, water, gold, iron, cotton)
  • Compound Nouns: Nouns formed by the combination of more than one part of speech. They consist of two or more than two words. (e.g. land + lord = landlord, sales + man = salesman)
  • Gender: Nouns can be classified into four genders: masculine, feminine, common and neuter.
Exercise A: Choose the correct common nouns from the box and complete these sentences.

[river, scientist, festival, mountain, plays, monument, city, museum]

1. Albert Einstein was a great ________.
2. The Amazon ________ flows through South America.
3. Diwali is a popular ________ in India.
4. Mount Everest stands as the tallest ________ on the planet.
5. Shakespeare wrote many famous ________.
6. The Taj Mahal is a beautiful ________.
7. My friend lives in a ________.
8. We visited the ________ in Paris.
Answers:
1. scientist
2. river
3. festival
4. mountain
5. plays
6. monument
7. city
8. museum

Exercise C: Read each sentence carefully and identify the concrete and abstract nouns.

1. The statue is made of marble.
2. Her bravery saved the day.
3. He has great wisdom.
4. The house was built with bricks.
5. They showed immense courage during the event.
6. She wore a necklace made of gold.
7. His kindness was appreciated by everyone.
8. The book was placed on the table.
Answers:
1. statue (concrete), marble (concrete)
2. bravery (abstract), day (abstract)
3. wisdom (abstract)
4. house (concrete), bricks (concrete)
5. courage (abstract), event (abstract)
6. necklace (concrete), gold (concrete)
7. kindness (abstract)
8. book (concrete), table (concrete)
