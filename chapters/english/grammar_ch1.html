Exercise D: Changing Gender
Rewrite these sentences by changing the gender. Make other changes if necessary.
1. The milkmaid met a wizard. → The milkman met a witch.
2. A gander is bigger than a drake. → A goose is bigger than a duck.
3. She was married to a great prince. → He was married to a great princess.
4. The groom is the son of a prominent landlord. → The bride is the daughter of a prominent landlady.
5. The old man had a little daughter. → The old woman had a little son.
6. The lady expressed her gratitude to my aunt for her assistance. → The gentleman expressed his gratitude to my uncle for his assistance.
7. The schoolmaster acted as the host in the party at his house. → The schoolmistress acted as the hostess in the party at her house.
8. The duke wishes to speak with his daughter. → The duchess wishes to speak with her son.

? Beyond the Textbook

E1. The hero saved the emperor from a tiger. → The heroine saved the empress from a tigress.
E2. The widower left his estate to his nephew. → The widow left her estate to her niece.
E3. The monk gave a blessing to the stallion. → The nun gave a blessing to the mare.
E4. The king asked the waiter for more tea. → The queen asked the waitress for more tea.
E5. My grandfather is a talented actor. → My grandmother is a talented actress.
