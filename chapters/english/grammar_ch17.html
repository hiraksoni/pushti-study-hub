Chapter 17: Active and Passive Voice

  • Active Voice: A verb is in the active voice when the subject does the action.
    - Example: Ronnie plays the guitar.
  • Passive Voice: The form of the verb which shows that the subject is the receiver of an action.
    - Example: The guitar is played by Ronnie.
  • Rules for Changing to Passive Voice:
    - The object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive sentence.
    - The subject of the active sentence becomes the agent, and we usually add 'by' before it.
    - An appropriate form of the verb 'be' is added before the main verb, which changes to the past participle (3rd form).
    - Only transitive verbs can be changed into passive.
  • Pronoun Changes:
    I → me | we → us | you → you | she → her | he → him | they → them
  • Tense Changes (Active → Passive):
    - Simple Present: teach → is/am/are taught
    - Present Continuous: is teaching → is/am/are being taught
    - Present Perfect: has taught → has/have been taught
    - Simple Past: taught → was/were taught
    - Past Continuous: was teaching → was/were being taught
    - Past Perfect: had taught → had been taught
    - Simple Future: will teach → will be taught
    - Future Perfect: will have taught → will have been taught
Exercise A: Fill in the blanks using the correct option.

1. The food ________ by the chef. (is prepared/prepares)
2. The car ________ by John. (was driven/drives)
3. The song ________ by the choir. (is sung/sings)
4. The project ________ by the team. (was completed/completes)
5. The letter ________ by him. (was written/writes)
6. The cake ________ by Mary. (is baked/bakes)
7. The house ________ by the architect. (was designed/designs)
8. The meeting ________ by the manager. (is chaired/chairs)
Answers:
1. is prepared
2. was driven
3. is sung
4. was completed
5. was written
6. is baked
7. was designed
8. is chaired
Exercise B: Transform these sentences by changing the voice.

1. He does not read the newspaper.
2. They are not organising the event.
3. She has not completed the assignment.
4. We did not attend the meeting.
5. The artist was not painting the mural.
6. They had not finished the work.
7. He will not accept the offer.
8. She may not have seen the movie.
Answers:
1. The newspaper is not read by him.
2. The event is not being organised by them.
3. The assignment has not been completed by her.
4. The meeting was not attended by us.
5. The mural was not being painted by the artist.
6. The work had not been finished by them.
7. The offer will not be accepted by him.
8. The movie may not have been seen by her.
