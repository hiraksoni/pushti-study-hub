Chapter 5: Adjectives

  • Adjectives: Words that qualify nouns or pronouns.
  • Attributive vs. Predicative:
    - Attributive: Used before the noun (e.g., A hardworking police officer).
    - Predicative: Used after a verb like be, feel, become, seem (e.g., The police officer is hardworking).
  • Kinds of Adjectives:
    - Quality: shape, size, colour (intelligent, brave).
    - Quantity: provide information about the quantity (enough, whole).
    - Interrogative: ask questions (What, Which).
    - Number: how many persons/things (five, some, several).
    - Demonstrative: point out people/things (This, That, These).
    - Possessive: show belonging (my, your).
    - Distributive: member of a group (Each, Neither).
    - Emphasising: lay stress (very, actual).
    - Proper: formed from proper nouns (Indian, Italian).
    - Compound: formed by combining two or more words (ice cold, brightly lit).
  • Degrees of Comparison:
    1. Positive: presence of a quality (big).
    2. Comparative: compares two things (bigger).
    3. Superlative: compares more than two things (biggest).
  • Order of Adjectives (OSASCOMP):
    When using more than one adjective, place them in this order:
    Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Colour → Origin → Material → Purpose
    Example: A beautiful (1) large (2) old (3) round (4) red (5) Italian (6) wooden (7) dining (8) table.
Exercise A: Underline the adjectives in these sentences. Identify whether they are attributive (A) or predicative (P).

1. The girl went with his obedient dog.
2. The cake looks delicious and fresh.
3. The beautiful girl walked off the ramp.
4. Your new business is flourishing well.
5. My mother helped me immensely.
6. The old man appeared wise and experienced to everyone.
7. Do you know an eminent politician?
8. The beast was ferocious.
Answers:
1. obedient (A)
2. delicious (P), fresh (P)
3. beautiful (A)
4. new (A)
5. My (A - Possessive adjective)
6. old (A), wise (P), experienced (P)
7. eminent (A)
8. ferocious (P)
Exercise B: Identify and underline the adjectives in these sentences. Write their kinds.

1. Whose pen is that?
2. You can use any of these rooms.
3. She hugged her beautiful mother.
4. My mother gave me some money.
5. Why are we going to a boring place?
6. There are seven days in a week.
7. We already have five pens; we do not need more.
8. Let's get some light snacks.
Answers:
1. Whose (Interrogative)
2. these (Demonstrative)
3. beautiful (Quality)
4. some (Quantity)
5. boring (Quality)
6. seven (Number)
7. five, more (Number)
8. some (Quantity), light (Quality)
Exercise C: Identify and say if these sentences have possessive pronouns (PP) or possessive adjectives (PA). Some may have both.

1. Are those her glasses?
2. I am going to get my hair cut from the salon across the street.
3. I do not recognise that book, so it is not mine.
4. Lots of our friends are going to settle outside the country.
5. This is ours.
6. One should be comfortable in one's own company.
7. The dog seemed asleep but its tail was still wagging.
8. That is not their house. Theirs has a green door.
Answers:
1. her (PA)
2. my (PA)
3. mine (PP)
4. our (PA)
5. ours (PP)
6. one's (PA)
7. its (PA)
8. their (PA), Theirs (PP)
Exercise D: Rewrite these sentences, changing the adjectives to their comparative and superlative forms as specified.

1. This cake is tasty. (comparative)
2. She is taller than her sister. (superlative)
3. This puzzle is easy. (superlative)
4. This car is more expensive than that one. (positive)
5. He is the fastest runner in the school. (comparative)
6. This book is interesting. (comparative)
7. That was the most difficult exam. (positive)
8. This story is more exciting than the previous one. (superlative)
Answers:
1. This cake is tastier.
2. She is the tallest.
3. This puzzle is the easiest.
4. This is an expensive car.
5. He is faster than that runner.
6. This book is more interesting than that book.
7. That was a difficult exam.
8. This story is the most exciting story.
Exercise E: Fill in the blanks with the correct order of adjectives given in brackets.

1. The Guptas live in a ________________ (spacious, three storey) bungalow.
2. When we went to the furniture market, I found a ________________ (wooden, antique) chair.
3. The art gallery is full of ________________ (beautiful, Asian, decorative) pieces.
4. We have a ________________ (large, old, lovely) house.
5. I love visiting ________________ (Greek, ancient, fascinating) ruins.
6. Last week, we decided to see a ________________ (famous, black-and-white, old) movie.
7. He adopted a ________________ (black, stray, small) puppy from the shelter.
8. She decided to wear a ________________ (blue, beautiful) dress to the party.
Answers:
1. spacious, three storey (Opinion, Size)
2. antique wooden (Age, Material)
3. beautiful Asian decorative (Opinion, Origin, Purpose)
4. lovely large old (Opinion, Size, Age)
5. fascinating ancient Greek (Opinion, Age, Origin)
6. famous old black-and-white (Opinion, Age, Colour)
7. small black stray (Size, Colour, Purpose/Type)
8. beautiful blue (Opinion, Colour)
