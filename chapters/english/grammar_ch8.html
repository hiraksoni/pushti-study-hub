Chapter 8: Verbs: Revision

  • Verbs: Essential components of sentences, indicating actions, occurrences, experiences or states of being.
  • Main and Auxiliary Verbs:
    - Main verbs: indicate the action of the subject (e.g., jump, ate).
    - Auxiliary verbs: helping verbs that indicate the tense of the main verb (e.g., be, have, do).
  • Kinds of Main Verbs:
    - Regular verbs: form simple past and past participle by adding -ed (walk → walked).
    - Irregular verbs: form past and past participles in other ways (burn → burnt).
    - Transitive verbs: require an object to complete their meaning. They answer What or Whom.
    - Intransitive verbs: do not require an object (e.g., The baby laughed).
  • Direct and Indirect Objects:
    - Direct objects: receive the action directly (answers 'What').
    - Indirect objects: receive the action indirectly (answers 'To Whom').
    Example: He sent his friend (Indirect) a message (Direct).
  • Complements: Words that complete the meaning of the predicate.
    - Subject complement: follows a linking verb and describes the subject (The boy is brave).
    - Object complement: follows and modifies the direct object (They elected him president).
  • Verbs of Incomplete Prediction: Verbs that require additional words to complete their meaning (e.g., seems, appears, might).
Exercise A: Choose the correct form of the main or auxiliary verbs.

1. What ________ you usually do on weekends? (do/does)
2. ________ they coming for the party? (Is/Are)
3. Sheena ________ baked some cookies. (have/has)
4. I ________ at the new restaurant yesterday. (was/were)
5. How long ________ you think the wait will be? (do/did)
6. They ________ been waiting for an hour. (have/has)
7. They ________ finished their homework. (has/have)
8. He ________ cooking dinner when I arrived. (is/was)
Answers:
1. do
2. Are
3. has
4. was
5. do
6. have
7. have
8. was
Exercise B: Underline and identify whether the verb is transitive (TV) or intransitive (IV).

1. The dog barked loudly.
2. She sings beautifully.
3. He wrote a novel.
4. They danced at the party.
5. The sun rose early.
6. We watched the movie.
7. She cried for hours.
8. He plays the guitar.
Answers:
1. barked (Intransitive - IV)
2. sings (Intransitive - IV)
3. wrote (Transitive - TV)
4. danced (Intransitive - IV)
5. rose (Intransitive - IV)
6. watched (Transitive - TV)
7. cried (Intransitive - IV)
8. plays (Transitive - TV)
Exercise C: Identify the direct (DO) and indirect objects (IO).

1. She lent her sister a book.
2. He brought me some flowers.
3. They offered the guests refreshments.
4. The teacher told the students a story.
5. She gave her dog a treat.
6. He showed us his new car.
7. She wrote her mother a letter.
8. They sent their friends invitations.
Answers:
1. DO: a book | IO: her sister
2. DO: some flowers | IO: me
3. DO: refreshments | IO: the guests
4. DO: a story | IO: the students
5. DO: a treat | IO: her dog
6. DO: his new car | IO: us
7. DO: a letter | IO: her mother
8. DO: invitations | IO: their friends
Exercise D: Identify the complement and its kind.

1. Seeva became a doctor.
2. They named their daughter Emily.
3. The room smells fresh.
4. He called his dog Max.
5. The food tastes spicy.
6. They considered the plan a success.
7. She felt happy.
8. The sky looks clear.
Answers:
1. a doctor (Subject Complement)
2. Emily (Object Complement)
3. fresh (Subject Complement)
4. Max (Object Complement)
5. spicy (Subject Complement)
6. a success (Object Complement)
7. happy (Subject Complement)
8. clear (Subject Complement)
