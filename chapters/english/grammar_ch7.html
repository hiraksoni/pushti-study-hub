Chapter 7: Articles

  • Articles: Words that point out the person or thing which is being referred to. Tells if mentioned in general or particular sense.
  • Indefinite Articles (a, an): Used before singular countable nouns.
    - a: before words starting with a consonant sound, or words beginning with 'e'/'u' that give a /y/ sound (a unanimous decision, a one-rupee note).
    - an: before words starting with a vowel sound, or words with silent initial consonants (an honest man).
  • Definite Article (the): Used when speaking about a particular person or thing. Used before singular countable, plural countable, and uncountable nouns.
    - When referring to a particular person/thing or one already mentioned.
    - When a singular noun indicates a whole class (The dog is a faithful animal).
    - Before unique things (the sun, the earth).
    - Before proper nouns in specific cases (rivers, oceans, holy books, famous buildings, etc.).
    - With adjectives before ordinals (the first) and superlatives (the best).
  • Omission of Articles: Articles are generally omitted before:
    - Proper nouns (Delhi, Shivaji)
    - Names of materials (wood, tea, coffee)
    - Abstract nouns in a general sense (Honesty, Health)
    - Arts and sciences (physics, painting)
    - Languages, games, meals, diseases, seasons, and days of the week.
Exercise A: Fill in the blanks with a/an.

1. Can we include ________ backflip in the dance routine?
2. Have you met him? He is ________ honourable man.
3. It would take me ________ hour to reach there.
4. I have ________ Master's degree in English.
5. You should use ________ umbrella when it is raining so hard.
6. I have never seen ________ European woman.
7. I think I have gained ________ few kilos after the holidays.
8. I have ________ older brother and ________ younger sister.
Answers:
1. a
2. an
3. an
4. a
5. an
6. a (European gives a /y/ sound)
7. a
8. an, a
Exercise B: Rewrite these sentences using the at proper places.

1. Sun sets in west.
2. Climate of this place does not suit me.
3. Taj Express is late.
4. Allahabad stands on bank of Ganga.
5. Gita is holy message of Lord Krishna.
6. Gardener is watering rose plants.
7. This is girl I met on Monday.
8. Horse is a noble animal.
Answers:
1. The Sun sets in the west.
2. The climate of this place does not suit me.
3. The Taj Express is late.
4. Allahabad stands on the bank of the Ganga.
5. The Gita is the holy message of Lord Krishna.
6. The gardener is watering the rose plants.
7. This is the girl I met on Monday.
8. The horse is a noble animal.

Answers:

2. an

5. the (superlative)
6. X
7. an
8. the, the
