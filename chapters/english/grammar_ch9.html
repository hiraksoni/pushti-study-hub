Chapter 9: Modals and Auxiliaries

  • Auxiliary Verbs: Helping verbs used with main verbs to complete the meaning (indicate tense, mood, voice).
  • Primary Auxiliaries: Can be used as main or auxiliary verbs.
    - do (do, does, did): form interrogative/negative sentences.
    - be (is, am, are, was, were): show state of being, form continuous tenses and passive voice.
    - have (has, have, had): form perfect/perfect continuous tenses.
  • Modal Auxiliaries (Modals): Show certainty, possibility, necessity, ability, or permission.
    - They do not change with number or person.
    - Never used alone (always with a main verb).
    - Cannot be used in continuous tenses.
    - Examples: may, can, could, must, shall, might, would, should, ought to.
  • Key Modals & Usage:
    - Shall / Will: express future, command, threat, promise, willingness.
    - Should: obligation, advice, probability.
    - Would: past habit, polite request, refusal.
    - Can / Could: ability, permission, general statement.
    - May / Might: permission, possibility, wish, purpose.
    - Must: necessity, strong obligation, certainty.
    - Ought (to): moral obligation, probability.
Exercise A: Fill in the blanks with the correct primary auxiliaries.

1. ________ you see him yesterday?
2. They ________ recognise him at all.
3. The girls ________ a fight yesterday.
4. The girl ________ a pink dress.
5. Geoffrey ________ coming back from London.
6. I ________ studying for an exam.
7. She excelled in her studies and so ________ her brother.
8. They ________ going to the party.
Answers:
1. Did
2. did not (didn't)
3. had
4. has
5. is
6. was (or am)
7. did
8. are (or were)
Exercise B: Fill in the blanks with appropriate modals.

1. ________ I borrow your car, madam? (Can, May)
2. Mohit ________ win the race. (might, could)
3. ________ you live long! (May, Can)
4. You ________ to obey orders. (must, ought, should)
5. Ravi ________ speak English well. (can, may)
6. You ________ exercise regularly to keep fit. (can, should, ought)
7. ________ you please help me in this matter? (Should, Could, May)
8. He is very ill. We ________ call a doctor. (would, should, must)
Answers:
1. May
2. might
3. May
4. ought
5. can
6. should
7. Could
8. must
Exercise C: Fill in the blanks using modals as directed in the brackets.

1. ________ you prosper, my son! (wish)
2. I am leaving for lunch. ________ you like to come? (polite request)
3. ________ you spare some time for me, please? (very polite request)
4. You ________ not have spoken these words. (advice)
5. We ________ run or we will miss the train. (necessity)
6. I ran as fast as I ________. (ability)
7. We ________ not give up fighting. (determination)
8. She asked me what I ________ do then. (desirability)
Answers:
1. May
2. Would
3. Could
4. should
5. must
6. could
7. will
8. should
Exercise D: Identify the modals in each of these sentences and state their functions.

1. She can speak three languages fluently.
2. You must finish your homework before you go out.
3. May I use your phone for a moment?
4. They might come to the party later.
5. He should apologise for his mistake.
6. We could see the mountains from our hotel room.
7. You ought to take better care of your health.
8. I would like a cup of tea, please.
Answers:
1. can (ability)
2. must (necessity / obligation)
3. May (permission)
4. might (possibility)
5. should (advice / obligation)
6. could (past ability)
7. ought (obligation)
8. would (polite request)

8. We ________ go to the beach if the weather is nice.
Answers:

2. May (or Can)
3. might (or could)
