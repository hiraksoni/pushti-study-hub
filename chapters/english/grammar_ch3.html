Chapter 3: Pronouns

  • Pronouns: Words used in place of nouns. They must agree with the nouns they replace in number and gender (e.g., I, we, you, he, she, his, her, their, them, myself, who, these).
  • Personal Pronouns: Replace names of persons and things.
    - First Person (speaker): I, we
    - Second Person (spoken to): you
    - Third Person (spoken about): he, she, it, they
    - Subjective: play the role of a subject.
    - Objective: play the role of an object.
  • Possessive Pronouns: Show possession or ownership (mine, ours, yours, his, hers, its, theirs).
  • Demonstrative Pronouns: Indicate or point out nouns in a sentence (this, that, these, those). This/these denote nearness, while that/those denote distance.
  • Interrogative Pronouns: Ask questions (who, which, what, whose, whom).
    - who/whom: for people
    - whose: possession
    - which: choice among persons/things
    - what: for things
  • Reflexive & Emphatic Pronouns: (myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves)
    - Reflexive: reflect upon or go back to the subject. Used after verbs and prepositions.
    - Emphatic: used to indicate action is done by a noun/pronoun. Used after nouns and pronouns.
  • Relative Pronouns: Join a phrase or clause with another in a sentence (who, whom, whose, which, that).
  • Distributive Pronouns: Denote persons or things taken as single (either, neither, every, each, both, all).
  • Reciprocal Pronouns: Show relationship between two subjects or objects (each other -> two people; one another -> more than two).
  • Indefinite Pronouns: Refer to unspecified beings, objects or places (someone, something, anyone, nobody, nowhere, everything, no one).
Exercise A: Identify and write the pronouns in these sentences as subjective or objective.

1. We will join you at the party tomorrow.
2. Can you help us with our homework?
3. I saw him at the park yesterday.
4. He looked at me and smiled.
5. The teacher asked if he had completed his assignment.
6. You are an intelligent person.
7. It was a beautiful day.
8. They enjoyed at the party.
Answers:
1. We (subjective), you (objective)
2. you (subjective), us (objective)
3. I (subjective), him (objective)
4. He (subjective), me (objective)
5. he (subjective) ["his" is possessive adj]
6. You (subjective)
7. It (subjective)
8. They (subjective)

Exercise C: Fill in the blanks with the correct pronouns.

1. This is my book. ________ belongs to me.
2. ________ is a beautiful sunset over the mountains.
3. I can't find my keys. Have you seen ________?
4. Look at that bird over there. ________ is building a nest.
5. These are my friends. ________ have been with me since childhood.
6. He forgot his umbrella at home. ________ will need it when it rains.
7. I know these students very well. ________ are all very hardworking.
8. This is your pen, isn't it? ________ is on the table.
Answers:
1. It
2. This (or That)
3. them
4. It
5. They
6. He
7. They
8. Mine
