Chapter 6: Determiners

  • Determiners: Words used before nouns to determine their meaning or point them out.
  • Kinds of Determiners: Articles, Possessives, Demonstratives, Quantifiers, Distributives, Interrogatives.
  • Articles:
    - a / an (Indefinite): Used for single countable nouns when not specific. 'an' is used before vowel sounds; 'a' is used before consonant sounds.
    - the (Definite): Used before both singular and plural nouns to denote something particular.
  • Possessives & Demonstratives:
    - Demonstratives: tell relative position (this, that, these, those).
    - Possessives: show relation or possession (my, our, your, its, their, his, her).
  • Quantifiers: tell us about number or quantity.
    - Many / several: used with countable nouns.
    - Much / little: used with uncountable nouns.
    - Some: used in positive sentences (and interrogative if expecting 'yes').
    - Any: used in negative sentences (and interrogative if expecting 'no').
    - A few: used with countable nouns.
  • Distributives: show distribution (either, neither, each, every).
    - Each: limited number, singular verb, emphasis on individual.
    - Every: large number, singular verb.
    - Either: anyone of the two.
    - Neither: not one or the other.
  • Interrogatives: help ask questions (which, who, why, whose, what, how much, how many).
Exercise A: Fill in the blanks with the correct articles.

1. ________ cow grazes in the field.
2. ________ elephant is a very big animal.
3. He is ________ best boy in the class.
4. The horse is ________ faithful animal.
5. ________ students of this school are smart.
6. Akash is ________ honest person.
7. ________ year has twelve months.
8. ________ speech that he made was inspiring.
Answers:
1. A (or The)
2. The (or An)
3. the
4. a
5. The
6. an
7. A
8. The
Exercise B: Fill in the blanks with correct possessives or demonstratives.

1. Jackson gave pizza to ________ sister.
2. I do not like ________ people at all.
3. Samantha and ________ family spent time in the Maldives.
4. When I had lost ________ phone, the man offered me his.
5. Is that ________ cat? Why is she outside the house?
6. Many of ________ friends are going to the mountains for vacation.
7. I like the flowers over there, but ________ flowers do not smell nice.
8. Put ________ books in the next room.
Answers:
1. his
2. these (or those)
3. her
4. my
5. your (or his/her)
6. my (or our/his/her)
7. these (or those)
8. those (or these)
Exercise C: Choose the correct options to complete these sentences with appropriate quantifiers.

1. I have ________ books on my shelf. (many/several)
2. She didn't drink ________ water. (much/little)
3. ________ students attended the lecture. (Few/A few)
4. We need ________ sugar for the recipe. (some/any)
5. There aren't ________ cookies left. (some/any)
6. ________ people like to travel during the holidays. (Most/Many)
7. ________ patience is needed to solve this puzzle. (Much/Little)
8. I bought ________ apples from the market. (a few/much)
Answers:
1. many (or several)
2. much
3. A few
4. some
5. any
6. Most
7. Much
8. a few
Exercise D: Choose the correct distributives given in the brackets.

1. ________ of the options is acceptable to me. (Either/Neither)
2. ________ student in the class has a unique talent. (Each/Every)
3. ________ of the two brothers is responsible. (Either/Neither)
4. ________ book on this shelf is interesting. (Every/Each)
5. ________ of the candidates could solve the problem. (Neither/Either)
6. ________ player on the team gave their best. (Every/Each)
7. ________ you choose, I will support. (Either/Neither)
8. ________ participant was awarded a certificate. (Each/Every)
Answers:
1. Neither
2. Every
3. Neither
4. Each (or Every)
5. Neither
6. Each (or Every)
7. Either
8. Each
