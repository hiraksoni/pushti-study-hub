Chapter 10: Finite and Non-Finite Verbs

  • Finite Verbs: Verbs that have a subject and show tense (past, present, or future). They can stand alone as the main verb in a sentence.
    - Example: She writes every day. (present tense)
    - Example: They went to the park. (past tense)
  • Non-Finite Verbs: Verbs that do not change according to the subject or show tense. They include infinitives (to + verb), gerunds (-ing forms acting as nouns), and participles.
    - They cannot stand alone as the main verb.
    - Example: She likes to read books. (infinitive)
    - Example: The broken vase needs to be fixed. (participle)
Exercise A: Fill in the blanks with the correct finite verbs.

1. She ________ to school every morning. (walk)
2. They ________ their grandparents last weekend. (visit)
3. He ________ for the exam tomorrow. (study)
4. I ________ my homework before dinner. (finish)
5. We ________ to the movie theatre tonight. (go)
6. She ________ a novel every Sunday. (read)
7. He ________ dinner yesterday. (cook)
8. They ________ television in the evening. (watch)
Answers:
1. walks
2. visited
3. will study (or is studying / studies)
4. finished
5. will go (or are going / went)
6. reads
7. cooked
8. will watch (or watched)
