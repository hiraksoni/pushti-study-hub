Finite Verbs

Textbook Definition: Verbs that have a subject and show tense (past, present, or future). They can stand alone as the main verb in a sentence.
Layman Terms: These verbs change based on "who" is doing the action or "when" it happened. If you change the subject from "I" to "He" or the time from "Today" to "Yesterday", the verb changes!

Non-Finite Verbs

Textbook Definition: Verbs that do not change according to the subject or show tense. They include infinitives (to + verb), gerunds (-ing forms acting as nouns), and participles. They cannot stand alone as the main verb.
Layman Terms: Verbs that are frozen! They never change no matter who is doing the action or what time it is. They pretend to be nouns or adjectives instead of a normal verb.
