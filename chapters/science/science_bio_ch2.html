Subjective Questions
Very Short Answer Type
1. When does the sudden and noticeable changes begin to appear in humans?
Answer: At the onset of puberty, which generally marks the beginning of adolescence (around 10-11 years of age).
9. What is the function of sweat glands?
Answer: Sweat glands secrete sweat to help regulate body temperature and excrete small amounts of waste. During puberty, they become highly active, often leading to body odor.
10. What is menopause?
Answer: Menopause is the permanent stoppage of the menstrual cycle in a female, typically occurring between 45 to 50 years of age, marking the end of the reproductive phase.
11. Testosterone and estrogen are secreted by which organs?
Answer: Testosterone is secreted by the testes in males, and estrogen is secreted by the ovaries in females.
Short Answer Type
3. Explain the development of sex organs in males and females.
Answer: During puberty, male sex organs like the testes mature completely and begin producing sperms. In females, the ovaries enlarge and start releasing mature eggs (ova). This marks reproductive maturity.
6. Why sanitary pads are preferred over cotton cloth?
Answer: Sanitary pads are far more hygienic, highly absorbent, and prevent the growth of harmful bacteria. Using unsterilized cotton cloth can lead to severe Reproductive Tract Infections (RTIs).
8. Write a short note on Adam's apple.
Answer: At puberty, the voice box (larynx) in boys begins to grow rapidly. This enlarged voice box can be seen as a protruding part of the throat called the Adam's apple. It causes the voice to become deep and hoarse.
Long Answer Type
1. Describe various mental, intellectual and emotional changes taking place during adolescence.
Answer:
Mental/Intellectual: Adolescents spend a lot of time thinking. Their intellectual capacity peaks, and they become more self-conscious and independent in their thought process.
Emotional: Due to hormonal surges, they often experience mood swings, alternating between extreme happiness and sadness. They may feel insecure as they try to adapt to sudden changes in their body and mind.
5. How can bad habits like drug addiction harm adolescents?
Answer: Drugs are highly addictive substances. When adolescents succumb to peer pressure and try drugs, they can become dependent. Addiction destroys physical health (damaging organs like the liver and brain), ruins academic performance, and severely harms social and family relationships. It often leads to a cycle of depression and anxiety.
