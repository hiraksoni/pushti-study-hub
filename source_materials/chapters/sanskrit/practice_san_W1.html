पत्रलेखन-कौशलम् • The Structure & Secrets of Sanskrit Letter Writing

CBSE Class 7 Writing Strategy

In CBSE Class 7 Sanskrit examinations, letter writing (पत्रलेखनम्) carries 5 full marks. You are provided with a complete letter frame with 10 blanks and a मञ्जूषा (Help Box) containing 10 words. Understanding the letter's anatomy and grammatical triggers guarantees a perfect 10/10 (100%) score!

Class 1
औपचारिक-पत्रम् (Formal Letters / प्रार्थनापत्राणि)
• Recipient: Principal (प्रधानाचार्य/प्रधानाचार्या), Teachers, School Authorities.
• Opening: श्रीमन्तः / माननीयाः प्रधानाचार्या महोदयाः, सविनयं निवेदनम् इदं यत्...
• Tone: Highly respectful, humble, concise, requesting leave or certificates.
• Closing: भवताम् आज्ञाकारी शिष्यः (for boys) / आज्ञाकारिणी शिष्या (for girls), धन्यवादः.
Class 2
अनौपचारिक-पत्रम् (Informal / Personal Letters)
• Recipient: Friends (मित्रम्), Parents (माता-पितरौ), Siblings (अनुज/अग्रज).
• Opening: प्रिय मित्र [नाम]!, असीम स्नेहः, अत्र कुशलं तत्रास्तु...
• Tone: Affectionate, warm, conversational, describing events or invitations.
• Closing: तव प्रियं मित्रम् / तव अभिन्नहृदयः, सर्वेभ्यः नमः.

The 5 Anatomy Anchors of Every Sanskrit Letter

Anchor 1
स्थानं तिथिः च
Top location & date: e.g. परीक्षाभवनात् (From Exam Hall), दिनांकः.
Anchor 2
सम्बोधनम्
Salutation: श्रीमन्तः (Formal) vs प्रिय मित्र (Informal).
Anchor 3
अभिवादनम्
Greeting: सादरं प्रणमामि (Elders) vs असीम स्नेहः / नमस्ते (Friends).
Anchor 4
कलेवरम् (Body)
Core message: अत्र कुशलं तत्रास्तु (Informal) vs सविनयं निवेदनम् (Formal).
Anchor 5
समापनम्
Ending: आज्ञाकारी शिष्यः (Formal) vs तव प्रियं मित्रम् (Informal).
The 4-Step Manjusha Cracking Strategy (मञ्जूषा-समाधान-चतुःसूत्री)
Step 1: Identify Letter Type — Is it addressed to the Principal (Formal) or a Friend (Informal)? This instantly fixes the salutation and conclusion.
Step 2: Lock Top & Bottom Anchors First — Blanks at the very top (e.g. परीक्षाभवनात्, श्रीमन्तः, प्रिय) and very bottom (धन्यवादः, आज्ञाकारी शिष्यः, मित्रम्) can be filled in 10 seconds.
Step 3: Check Grammatical Clues in the Body —
  • If you see सह, search the Manjusha for a 3rd case noun (e.g. जनकेन सह, परिवारेण सह, मित्रैः सह).
  • If you see स्निह्, search for a 7th case noun (e.g. छात्रेषु स्निह्यति).
  • If you see अहम्, look for a 1st-person singular verb (e.g. पठामि, गमिष्यामि, करोमि).
Step 4: Re-read with Translation — Translate the sentences mentally into Hindi or English to ensure every line makes flawless logical sense.
Letter 1 • अनौपचारिक-पत्रम् (Informal to Friend)

१. स्वसंस्कृत अध्यापिकायाः वर्णनं कुर्वन् मित्रं प्रति पत्रं लिखत।

Writing a letter to friend Anjika describing one's Sanskrit teacher, Smt. Krishna Mishra.
Learning Pointers & Grammatical Clues:
  • अभिधानम्: Means "Name / Title" (अभिधानं कृष्णामिश्रा अस्ति = Name is Krishna Mishra).
  • स्निह्यति Rule: The root verb स्निह् (to love / care for) strictly commands the Locative Case (सप्तमी विभक्तिः) → सा छात्रेषु स्निह्यति (She loves the students).
  • Feminine Adjectives: Since the teacher is female, adjectives must take feminine endings: कुशला (expert), विनम्रा (polite/humble), मार्गदर्शिका (female mentor/guide).
  • अत्र कुशलं तत्रास्तु: The iconic Sanskrit informal greeting: "All is well here, may it be so there too!"

परीक्षा भवनात्
दिनांक : 11/8/26
प्रिय मित्र अञ्जिके !
असीम स्नेहः।

अत्र कुशलं (१) तत्रास्तु । अस्मिन् पत्रे (२) अहम् निजसंस्कृत अध्यापिकायाः (३) वर्णनम् करोमि। मम संस्कृत अध्यापिकायाः अभिधानं (४) कृष्णामिश्रा अस्ति। तस्याः स्वभावः अतीव (५) विनम्रा वर्तते । सा निजविषये (६) कुशला अस्ति । सा सदैव सत्यं वदति। सा (७) छात्रेषु स्निह्यति । सा मम (८) मार्गदर्शिका अस्ति। अहं पुनः मिलित्वा विस्तरेण कथयिष्यामि ।

सर्वेभ्यः (९) नमः ।

तव प्रियं (१०) मित्रम्
अञ्जिमा ।
Complete English Translation (आङ्ग्ल-भाषानुवादः):
Examination Hall
Date: 11/8/26
Dear friend Anjika,
Boundless love and affection.
All is well here, may it be so there too (तत्रास्तु). In this letter, I (अहम्) am presenting a description (वर्णनम्) of my Sanskrit teacher. My Sanskrit teacher's name is Krishna Mishra (कृष्णामिश्रा). Her nature is extremely polite and humble (विनम्रा). She is highly expert/skilled (कुशला) in her subject. She always speaks the truth. She is deeply affectionate towards the students (छात्रेषु). She is my guide/mentor (मार्गदर्शिका). When we meet again, I will tell you more in detail.
Salutations/Respects (नमः) to everyone at home.
Your dear friend (मित्रम्),
Anjima.

(२)अहम्First-person subject agreeing with the verb करोमि (लट् उत्तमपुरुष एकवचनम्).

(४)कृष्णामिश्राProper noun specifying the teacher's name (अभिधानम्).
(५)विनम्राFeminine adjective qualifying the teacher's humble disposition (स्वभावः).
(६)कुशलाFeminine adjective denoting expertise in her subject (निजविषये कुशला).
(७)छात्रेषुLocative plural (सप्तमी बहुवचनम्) governed by the verb of affection स्निह्यति.
(८)मार्गदर्शिकाFeminine noun meaning 'female mentor/guide'.

Letter 2 • औपचारिक-प्रार्थनापत्रम् (Formal Leave Application)

२. दिनद्वयस्य अवकाशार्थं प्रधानाचार्यां प्रति पत्रं लिखत।

Formal leave application to the Principal requesting 2 days leave of absence for a trip to Lucknow with father.
Learning Pointers & Grammatical Clues:
  • Formal Salutation: Always begins with honorific title श्रीमन्तः प्रधानाचार्या महोदयाः (Respected Principal Madam).
  • जनकेन सह (3rd Case): The companion keyword सह commands the Instrumental Case (तृतीया विभक्तिः) → जनकेन सह (with father).
  • दिनद्वयस्य अवकाशम्: Genitive compound meaning "leave of two days" (दिनद्वयस्य = of 2 days, अवकाशम् = leave/vacation).
  • उपकुर्वन्तु (Imperative Polite): दत्त्वा उपकुर्वन्तु = Having granted, please oblige me!
  • आज्ञाकारी शिष्यः: Standard formal student sign-off (Obedient student).

Letter 3 • अनौपचारिक-पत्रम् (Informal to Friend)

३. विद्यालयस्य खेलसमारोहस्य वर्णनं कुर्वन् मित्रं प्रति पत्रं लिखत।

Writing a letter to friend Shashansh describing the annual school Sports Day and winning the running race.
Learning Pointers & Grammatical Clues:
  • अभिन्नहृदय (Salutation): Literally "undivided heart" = dearest intimate friend.
  • धावने प्रथमः: In running (Locative Case / सप्तमी विभक्तिः of धावन).
  • पुरस्कारवितरणम् / पारितोषिकानि: Award distribution / medals and prizes.
  • स्वपत्रे लिख: Imperative order: "write about your sports events in your own letter!" (स्वपत्रे = in your letter).

Letter 4 • औपचारिक-प्रार्थनापत्रम् (Transfer Certificate - TC)

४. स्थानान्तरण प्रमाणपत्रं प्राप्तुं प्रधानाचार्यां प्रति पत्रं लिखत।

Formal application to the Principal requesting a School Transfer Certificate (TC) due to father's transfer to Dibrugarh, Assam.
Learning Pointers & Grammatical Clues:
  • स्थानान्तरण-प्रमाणपत्रम् (TC): School Transfer Certificate. स्थान + अन्तरणम् (transfer of location).
  • Tense Contrast: Note the grammatical contrast between current status (पठामि — Present Tense) and future schooling in Dibrugarh (पठिष्यामि — Future Tense).
  • परिवारेण सह: Once again, सह commands the 3rd Case (Instrumental / तृतीया) → परिवारेण सह (along with family).
  • प्रदाय अनुगृह्णन्तु: प्रदाय (having granted, ल्यप् प्रत्ययः) + अनुगृह्णन्तु (please favor/oblige).

Letter 5 • अनौपचारिक-पत्रम् (Birthday Invitation)

Birthday invitation letter to friend Piyush inviting him and his family to the celebrations on Sunday.
Learning Pointers & Grammatical Clues:
  • सार्धपञ्चवादने: Time expression meaning "at half past five (5:30 PM)". सार्ध = with half, पञ्च = 5, वादने = at ... o'clock.
  • मित्रैः सह (3rd Case Plural): Companion word सह commands 3rd case plural → मित्रैः सह (in the company of friends).
  • शोभां वर्धस्व: Cultural invitation phrase: "Please grace the occasion with your presence!" (शोभाम् = charm/splendor, वर्धस्व = enhance).
  • निर्धारिते समये: At the designated/fixed time (Locative Case / सप्तमी विभक्तिः).
