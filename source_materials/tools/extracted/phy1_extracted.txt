Introduction 13/4/26
As soon as we press the switch of a torch ON, it starts glowing. For the torch bulb to glow,
there must be something flowing in the wires. This 'something' must have energy, otherwise
it cannot produce heat and light energy in the torch bulb. We call this 'something' as electric
current. An electric current has electrical energy. This electrical energy gets converted into light
and heat energy when the current flows through an electric bulb. The electric current flows from
the end of the cell marked '+' (called the positive terminal) through the metal wires, to the end
marked' (called the negative terminal). The arrangement where an electric current flows in
a conductor when it is connected to a source of electric current is known as an electric circuit.
In this chapter, we will study about electric components and devices which are represented
by symbols in a circuit diagram, heating effect of current and magnetic effects of current. In
magnetic effects of current we will deal with magnetic effects by current flowing in a wire.

A Torchlight
A torch, or flashlight, is a portable electric light that works by converting stored electrical
energy from a battery into light. An electric torch works by completing an electric circuit when
the switch is turned on, allowing electricity to flow from the batteries to a bulb or LED. This
current makes the bulb's filament (or the LED) glow, producing light. A reflector and lens then
focus and direct the light, creating a beam.
Construction: A torch contains the following basic parts:
Battery: This is the power source. It stores chemical energy and turns it into electrical
energy. It has a positive (+) and a negative (-) end.

Incandescent bulb: Contains a thin filament, usually made of tungsten, that glows
when heated by the electric current.

Electromagnet
An electromagnet is based on the magnetic effect of current. It consists of a long length of
insulated copper wire coiled around an iron rod or a bar. When current is passed through this
coil, the iron rod behaves like a magnet. The magnetism of the iron rod disappears as soon as
the current through the coil is switched off. Therefore, an electromagnet is a temporary magnet.
Along with the current, the magnetism can also be switched 'OFF' or 'ON'.
The Factors on which the Strength of an Electromagnet depends
The strength of an electromagnet depends directly upon
the amount of current flowing through the coil.
the number of turns in the coil.
The strength of electromagnet also depends inversely upon the length of the electromagnet,
which means lesser the length of electromagnet, more will be the strength of electromagnet.

Electrical Conductors and Insulators
Conductors: Materials that allow electric current to pass through are called conductors. Metals
are good conductors. Examples: silver, copper, and gold.
Insulators: Materials that do not allow electric current to pass through are called insulators or
poor conductors of electricity. Examples: plastic, rubber, and ceramics.
Use of Conductors and Insulators
Metals like copper are used for making electrical wires because they are good conductors and
relatively inexpensive. Insulators (like plastic, rubber, and ceramics) are used to cover wires
because they stop electricity from passing through and keeps us safe from electric shocks.
The human body is a conductor of electricity Electric current passing through the body can
cause severe injury or death. Always handle electrical appliances with care. Avoid touching
switches or plugs with wet hands, and never use electrical devices in wet areas or with damaged
insulation or broken plugs.

gradually reduces, the superconductor's resistance drops
to zero below a fixed temperature, which is the critical
temperature. At this temperature, a superconductor can
conduct electricity with no resistance, which means no heat,
sound, or other forms of energy would be discharged from
the material when it reaches the "critical temperature" (Tc).

Introduction
We often use the word 'hot' and 'cold' while describing things around us. Suppose you have
a bowl of hot soup or a cup of coffee that is too hot. What can you do to cool it? You would
probably add something cooler such as ice.
When we mix ice to hot coffee it becomes cooler and ice starts melting. Something is getting
transferred between the ice and hot coffee.
 
This something is actually a kind of energy which
transfers from a hot body to a cold body and is known as heat. This chapter is an attempt to
elaborate different aspects of heat, temperature, their measurement and their effect.
Heat and Temperature
Heat is a form of energy which flows from a body at a
 
higher temperature to lower temperature,
Temperature
 
is a physical quantity that measures the degree of hotness or coldness of a body.
Heat can increase or decrease the temperature The direction of flow
 
of heat between two bodies
is determined by their temperature.
DO YOU
KNOW? Chemical energy stored in fuels or food can be changed into heat energy.
For example, burning wood, coal or candle releases heat and even the food we
eat gives energy that our body partly use as heat.
