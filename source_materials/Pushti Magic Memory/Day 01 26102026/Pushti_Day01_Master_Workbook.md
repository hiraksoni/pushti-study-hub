## 🧠 Part 1: The Core Brain Science (Decoded for Pushti)

### 1. Active Recall vs. Passive Input
* **The Trap:** Reading notes 10 times gives an illusion of learning. In exams, that data disappears.
* **The Rule:** True neural encoding happens during **Active Recall** (retrieving the answer with the book closed). Spend 20% of time picturing, 80% recalling.

### 2. The V.A.K. Event Formula (Visual - Auditory - Kinesthetic)
* **V (Visual):** Exaggerated 3D shapes, glowing bright neon colors.
* **A (Auditory):** Cartoon sound effects (*CRASH, BOOM, SQUEAK*).
* **K (Kinesthetic):** Physical weight, heat, cold, textures, and gut feelings.

### 3. Brain Filter Plates (Deletion, Distortion, Generalization)
Pushti's notes: *"distrust, detect, destroy, generalize, brain filter plates"*.
* The brain destroys normal, boring data.
* To make information bypass the filter into permanent **Reference Data**, the mental picture must be absurd, hilarious, and action-packed!
