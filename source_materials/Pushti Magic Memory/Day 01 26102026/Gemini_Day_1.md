## 1. Core Principles of Memory & The Brain

### A. Input vs. Recall
* **Class Insight:** *"All input is never the answer; recalling is very important."*
* Passive reading and re-reading create the illusion of competence. True memory strength occurs when the brain is forced to retrieve information without visual cues.

### B. The 3 Sensory Doors (The VAK Model)
1. **Vision ($V$):** Sight, pictures, shapes, colors.
2. **Hearing ($A$):** Sounds, speech, rhythms, auditory cues.
3. **Kinesthetic ($K$):** Feelings, physical sensations, textures, motion, and emotion.
* **The Formula:** Combining $V + A + K$ turns abstract data into a **Mental Event**, which the brain stores naturally.

### C. Brain Filter Plates (Deletion, Distortion, Generalization)
* The human brain encounters millions of bits of data daily.
* To prevent sensory overload, internal filters automatically:
  1. **Delete (Destroy):** Discard ordinary, routine, boring facts.
  2. **Distort (Detect/Shift):** Recast events to match current emotions or assumptions.
  3. **Generalize:** Lump repeated data into blanket rules.
* **Class Rule:** Ordinary text is erased by the filter. Information must be converted into surprising, vivid, or funny imagery to register as permanent **Reference Data**.

## 2. The 3 Steps to "Photogenic" (Photographic) Memory

1. **Step 1: Words to Thinking (Concept)** — Grasping the meaning behind the word.
2. **Step 2: Words to Picture (Snapshot)** — Immediately forming an image when hearing a word.
3. **Step 3: Improvement of Visualization** — Adding vibrant motion, scale, and sensory feel (taught progressively across subsequent days).

## 1. Deep Dive into the Techniques

### A. The Linking Method (Chain Technique)
* **How it works:** Item 1 connects to Item 2, Item 2 connects to Item 3, and so on.
* **Why it must not be written out:** Writing out the storyline converts a visual memory task back into linguistic rote processing. Keeping the storyline mental forces the visual cortex to render the sequence as episodic memory.
* **The Forward-Backward Test:** When images are properly linked, Pushti will be able to recite the list from 1 to 15, and immediately recite it backwards from 15 down to 1.

### B. The S.E.E. Principle for Associations
Normal mental pictures fade. To bypass the brain's "Deletion" filter, images should adhere to:
* **S — Sensory (VAK):** Hear the noise, smell the object, feel physical contact.
* **E — Exaggeration / Absurdity:** Make items gigantic, bizarre, or disproportionate.
* **E — Energetic Action:** Items must collide, smash, pour, or interact dynamically.
