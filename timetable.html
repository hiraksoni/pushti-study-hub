Kaushal Bodh (कौशल बोध)

Grade 7 Activity Book • NCERT NEP 2020

Kaushal Bodh introduces students to foundational vocational crafts and practical handiwork without academic exam stress:

  • Work Form 1 (Agriculture & Environment): Gardening, seed preservation, soil composting.
  • Work Form 2 (Materials & Engineering): Woodcraft, paper fabrication, wire joining, basic electrical safety.
  • Work Form 3 (Domestic & Human Services): Nutrition, tailoring, bookbinding, community service.
